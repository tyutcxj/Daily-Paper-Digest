Inventa: Decentralized Self-Improving Agents for Autonomous Scientific Discovery
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.51)

作者：Prithwish Jana, Nikos Kanakaris, Purak Jain, Anoop Deoras, Sahika Genc

发布日期：Wed,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2611.21847v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents can generate research ideas and improve scientific discovery through iterative experimentation. However, increasing the number of agents or iterations does not automatically translate to producing more diverse or feasible ideas. Agents may converge on similar candidates, propagate unsupported claims through shared memory components and repeatedly apply search strategies that no longer yield any improvements. We introduce Inventa, a framework for automated scientific discovery that employs autonomous research units and verified shared knowledge. Each unit creates a lineage of candidate ideas using a mutator agent. A local orchestrator agent periodically adapts the mutator's harness. When progress stalls, the orchestrator agent replaces its model or restarts the idea evolution process. Verifiers evaluate each candidate idea and record verified improvements, useful components and constraint violations in a shared knowledge, the only channel between units. We evaluate Inventa on five benchmarks spanning the research cycle, covering retrieval (ScholarCatalyst), ideation (IdeaBench), proposal writing (IdeaScientist), and scientific law discovery (EurekaBench, SciLaws-Bench). It leads all five, achieving 73.6% on EurekaBench versus 56.2% for CORAL. On IdeaBench, it discovers 26.0 novel ideas per paper, compared with 18.6 for ResearchAgent, with 82% fewer model calls.

AI 总结

研究问题：LLM agents在科学发现中易收敛、传播未验证主张、重复无效策略。 主要方法：Inventa框架，使用自主研究单元、变异体代理、本地协调器、验证器和共享知识。 关键贡献：在五个基准上领先，EurekaBench达73.6% vs 56.2%。

Can an LLM dream? Testing the overfitted brain hypothesis on an agent with text memory
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Eric Zou

发布日期：Wed,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2611.21648v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Hoel (2021) proposed that dreams distort replayed experience so that the brain does not overfit to recent events. We test this hypothesis on an LLM agent (Claude Opus 5.5) that stores what it learns in a text memory and rewrites that memory each night from the day's conversation transcript. The agent goes through ten simulated days of conversation built from LongMemEval. We corrupt 5, 10, 20 or 40 percent of the transcript's turns before each nightly rewrite in one of two ways. Detail corruption changes one or two details in a turn to plausible wrong ones, or replaces the turn with one by the same speaker from an earlier day. Random-text corruption replaces half of the selected turns with a passage of Moby-Dick and inserts Moby-Dick words into the other half. A size limit on the memory, rising from 19,500 to 117,000 tokens over ten nights, set from human free-recall data, forces it to forget. We learned that corruption did not help the agent apply what it learned in earlier conversations to new tasks in any of 30 runs (ten conditions, three different conversation histories). Replacing details in 40 percent of turns lowered recall from 76 to 60 percent. Random text had no consistent effect on recall, with a paired difference of 0 $\pm$ 2 points at 40 percent, because the model recognized it as corrupted and left it out of memory. Replaced details were stored as fact even when another turn held the correct value, and the agent answered 40 percent of those questions correctly, compared with 75 percent without corruption. These results suggest that a pretrained model can separate corrupted text from real events, so a fair test needs a learner that cannot make this separation as easily, such as a model fine-tuned on the corrupted days or an agent whose days take place in an invented world.

AI 总结

研究问题：测试梦境防止大脑过拟合的假说是否适用于LLM agent。 主要方法：在LLM夜间重写记忆时，对对话记录进行细节或随机文本干扰，观察对后续任务的影响。 关键贡献：发现干扰并未帮助agent应用先前知识，反而降低回忆率，不支持该假说。

EgoLink: Egocentric Language-Vision Interactive Network Knowledge Challenge Summary
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.48)

作者：Renhe Sun, Yueying Feng, Bohan Yu, Tong Niu, Yunqi Liu, Zitong Wang, Haolin He, Kexin Shi, Hualei Wang, Qiuqiang Kong, Chang Yao, Yuge Huang, Weiqiang Wang, Jingyuan Chen, Jian Liu

发布日期：Wed,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.21690v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The EgoLink Challenge at ACM Multimedia 2026 benchmarks egocentric multimodal intelligence linking first-person perception, social reasoning, and interactive action. Unlike benchmarks centered on visible objects and activities, EgoLink evaluates whether models can interpret emotional and social cues, resolve visually grounded references, and convert user requests into verified tool-mediated actions. Its two tracks cover social reasoning in egocentric video and interactive agents that complete service tasks through dialogue, tool use, and closed-loop execution. Together, they test whether perception, reasoning, and decision-making remain coupled under both offline question answering and state-changing interaction. We highlight a multi-stage Track 1 data pipeline for multiple-choice question (MCQ) generation, chain-of-thought generation, automatic quality grading, and video-contribution filtering. We summarize the task design, evaluation metrics, rules, leaderboards, and representative solutions. The benchmark combines objective answer scoring with process- and result-based evaluation, enabling analysis of both final correctness and execution quality. The best Track 1 system achieved 0.9263 accuracy, while the best Track 2 system achieved 0.844 joint success and 0.916 micro accuracy. Participant reports reveal a shared principle: strong systems make intermediate evidence explicit through candidate verification, option-robust reasoning, scenario-aware planning, tool canonicalization, and state-aware correction before answering or acting. These findings highlight the importance of process verification and evidence-grounded reasoning for reliable egocentric multimodal intelligence.

研究问题：评估第一人称多模态智能在社交推理与交互动作中的表现。 主要方法：构建MCQ生成、思维链、质量分级的数据管道，设计双赛道评估。 关键贡献：提出EgoLink基准，结合客观评分与过程结果评估，测试感知、推理与决策耦合。
