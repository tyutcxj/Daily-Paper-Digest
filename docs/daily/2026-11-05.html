When Does Sharing Work? Scaling a Shared SigLIP2 Across Image, Video, Audio, and Audio-Visual Representations
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.55)

作者：Xiaofei Sun, Peng Wang, Huayao Liu, Xiaoxiao Xu, Jun Song, Deqing Li, Lin Qu

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.05406v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Contrastive pretraining has advanced image-text understanding, yet image, video, and audio encoders are still trained independently, with cross-modal alignment realized mainly through post-hoc binding to a frozen anchor space. We present Omni-Encoder, a general multimodal encoder that processes image, video, audio, and audio-visual inputs within a single SigLIP-2 Vision Transformer with fully shared parameters. Unlike anchor-centric binding or per-modality adapters, all modalities are optimized jointly end-to-end, and the framework is organized from input to training. A modality-native input stage first maps each signal into the shared embedding space, including a 1D-convolutional audio tokenizer that converts mel-spectrograms into tokens aligned with visual patches. A multimodal rotary position embedding parameterized by absolute timestamps then places visual frames and audio segments in a unified spatiotemporal coordinate system. Finally, alternating-modality training interleaves image, video, audio, and temporally aligned audio-visual batches and accumulates their gradients into one update, which we find central to stable joint optimization. Omni-Encoder achieves competitive results on 16 benchmarks across all four modalities with one shared parameter set, and joint training improves each modality over its separately trained counterpart. Code and models will be publicly released.

AI 总结

研究问题：现有图像、视频、音频编码器独立训练，跨模态对齐依赖后处理绑定。 主要方法：提出Omni-Encoder，用共享参数的SigLIP-2 Vision Transformer端到端联合优化所有模态，包括模态原生输入、多模态旋转位置编码和交替模态训练。 关键贡献：在16个基准上取得竞争性结果。

Prospective Memory for Proactive LLM Agents
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.51)

作者：Yuanchen Bei, Zhining Liu, Tianxin Wei, Weizhi Zhang, Zhichen Zeng, Philip S. Yu, Hendrik Hamann, Jingrui He, Hanghang Tong

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2611.05731v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Most memory systems for LLM agents are retrospective, preserving and retrieving past information to inform current decisions. Proactive agents, however, also require prospective memory: retaining future-directed intentions and realizing them when appropriate opportunities arise, even without explicit requests. Crucially, remembering the right intention does not guarantee acting at the right time: a related observation may call for waiting, while a later cue may make the same intention actionable. Inspired by cognitive theories of human prospective memory, we introduce Pro-Memory, a memory framework that separates maintaining future intentions from deciding when and how to realize them. Pro-Memory preserves pending objectives linked to their supporting evidence, assesses new observations against their original goals and activation conditions, and coordinates appropriate contributions with the agent's ongoing actions. It further updates intention states based on actions actually issued or evidence of resolution, while an experience memory distills feedback from completed episodes into guidance for future decisions. Experiments on two benchmarks spanning delayed-intention tasks and open-ended proactive assistance demonstrate consistent improvements in proactive task performance over representative memory approaches across multiple LLM backbones, while limiting unnecessary interventions.

AI 总结

研究问题：现有LLM代理缺乏前瞻性记忆，无法自主记住并适时执行未来意图。 主要方法：提出Pro-Memory框架，分离意图维护与执行时机决策，结合经验记忆更新。 关键贡献：在延迟意图和开放任务上显著提升代理的主动性和时机把握能力。

AGORA: Reliability-Aware Aggregation of LLM Judges via Item-Response Theory
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.50)

作者：Junhyung Ahn

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.05556v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LLM-as-a-judge is a common substitute for costly human evaluation, and aggregating several judges is the standard remedy for any single judge's bias. Judge pools mix judges of very different reliability, while the human-labeled sets that validate them hold a few hundred items or fewer. In this regime the per-item mean weights weak judges as heavily as strong ones, and confusion-table models such as Dawid-Skene must estimate a per-judge error table from too few items. We propose AGORA (Aggregation Of Reliability-weighted Assessments), an item-response-theory aggregator that places every item on one latent quality scale and describes each judge by a sensitivity parameter and ordered rating thresholds, so judge reliability is learned from all items jointly and without human labels. Across three benchmarks, AGORA matches or exceeds Dawid-Skene on large evaluation sets, and its advantage widens as the set shrinks toward a few hundred items. It remains accurate as pools grow more uneven, a label-free dispersion statistic indicates in advance when trust weighting helps, and its learned trust weights track per-judge human validity.

AI 总结

研究问题：LLM法官聚合时可靠性不均，小数据集下均值法和混淆表模型效果差。 主要方法：提出AGORA，基于项目反应理论，联合学习法官敏感度与阈值，无需人工标签。 关键贡献：小数据集上优势显著，处理不均匀法官池，提供无标签分散统计量指示信任加权有效性。

Challenge-Aware Knowledge Recruitment with Meta-Agent Supervision for Medical Multi-Agent Reasoning
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.48)

作者：Riccardo Riccio, Haitz S\'aez de Oc\'ariz Borde, Pietro Li\`o

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.05393v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models perform strongly on standard medical QA but degrade on harder reasoning tasks, where multi-agent debate often fails to beat strong single-model prompting. We argue that a key limitation is that existing systems select experts and retrieve knowledge from the question alone, rather than from gaps that emerge during reasoning. We propose challenge-aware adaptive recruitment: an external CHALLENGER inspects the evolving reasoning trace and identifies specific factual or expertise gaps. These signals trigger targeted knowledge retrieval or mid-discussion specialist recruitment. On the MEDAGENTSBENCH hard subset (862 questions across nine biomedical datasets), upfront retrieval provides little benefit, while challenge-aware in-between retrieval performs better; on a multi-specialist backbone it improves accuracy on 8 of 9 datasets and adds 6 percentage points over Challenger Only. Ablations indicate benefits from both retrieval timing and gap-specific query signals, and a live web-search experiment shows that the timing advantage persists across retrieval backends. For specialist recruitment, self-triggered adaptation is unreliable, whereas Challenger-triggered recruitment raises GPT-4.1-mini MedQA accuracy from 46% to 54%, with positive direction on all tested generalization datasets. Naively combining retrieval and specialist recruitment degrades performance, highlighting the need to coordinate adaptive intervention channels. We give a closed-form condition for when no threshold on that signal makes a tiered cascade worthwhile relative to sending every query to the backstop.
