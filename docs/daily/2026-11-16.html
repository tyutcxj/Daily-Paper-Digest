More Agents, More Samples, or More Thinking? Untangling Coordination, Ensembling, and Test-Time Compute in Multi-Agent LLMs
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.57)

作者：Seyed Mahmoud Sajjadi Mohammadabadi

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.17610v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Evaluations of multi-agent language models often compare communicating architectures against a single greedy pass. This practice confounds coordination with sample multiplicity and inference compute. We synthesize nine primary empirical evaluations comprising 516 unique multi-agent configurations organized along a resource vector R with six dimensions and a baseline ladder with four categories. Against baselines with a single pass (Category A), multi-agent accuracy differences are negative overall and near zero when omitting the largest contributing study. When evaluated against baselines matched on sample count (Category B) or resource budget (Category C), multi-agent systems average -0.042 in accuracy, and multilevel random effects modeling on the log odds scale remains negative across sensitivity checks. Multi-agent accuracy equals or falls below the baseline in 74% of these comparable contrasts, and study means are nonpositive in almost all evaluated studies. Across studies reporting both baselines, moving from a greedy pass to a sampled baseline shifts accuracy differences by -0.038 on average. Because communication adds no information when agents share identical evidence, positive returns require partitioned evidence, external verification, or error diversity across models. We provide a reviewer checklist and accounting utilities.

AI 总结

研究问题：多智能体LLM评估混淆协调、样本多样性和推理计算，需解耦这些因素。 主要方法：综合9项实证研究，沿资源向量和基线阶梯对比516种多智能体配置。 关键贡献：匹配资源后多智能体准确率平均-0.042，74%对比低于基线，协调本身无收益。

AscendGYM: A Cost-Aware Kernel Optimization Benchmark for Real-World Workloads on Ascend NPUs
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.53)

作者：AI Training Platform Team, Bingwei Zhang, Jing Li, Amir Abdullin, Sihang Chen, Xin Chen, Liangjun Feng, Yingmeng Gao, Tao Guo, Junjie Huang, Dongfang Li, Zhanglian Lin, Jiajun Liu, Zekun Liu, Zhengxuan Lu, Xiaodong Luo, Jian Meng, Ivan Nosov, Linyuan Qiu, Weijian Sun, Wanrui Yan, Binfan Zheng, Zhicheng Zhou, Baotian Hu, Min Zhang, Haizhou Li, Zhiquan Luo

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.17621v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Kernel optimization is essential for efficient large language model (LLM) training and inference on Ascend NPUs. Existing Ascend kernel benchmarks primarily focus on kernel generation, with less emphasis on the ability of agents to optimize existing implementations under input configurations drawn from real workloads. To address this requirement, we introduce AscendGYM, a benchmark whose initial suite comprises 48 kernels across three difficulty levels. Each kernel is paired with test cases whose data types and tensor shapes are collected from real-world training and inference tasks. Consequently, performance improvements on AscendGYM map directly to the corresponding workloads. In terms of usability, two evaluation modes are supported in AscendGYM: model evaluation and model-harness evaluation. The kernel optimization capabilities of both trained models and custom agents are allowed to be measured. It provides a complete execution environment that closely reproduces how Ascend kernels are invoked in practical pipelines. For model evaluation, it additionally includes a built-in harness equipped with search orchestration and experience accumulation. Finally, we propose a new metric, the Speedup-Token Score (STS), which quantifies performance gains relative to token cost. By jointly considering speedup and cost, STS enables the assessment of practical kernel optimization capability of a model or harness under an acceptable budget. AscendGYM is available at https://github.com/SLAI-AITP/AscendGYM.

See Every Block Once: Variance-Reduced Post-Training for Masked Diffusion Language Models
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.47)

作者：Yunzhen He, Hiroaki Yamagiwa, Hidetoshi Shimodaira

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2611.17754v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Policy gradient methods are widely used to align large language model outputs with human preferences, but the log-likelihoods they require cannot be directly computed in Masked Diffusion Language Models (MDLMs). MDLM post-training therefore relies on a surrogate objective based on the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO), and recent work estimates a blockwise variant of this objective via Monte Carlo methods to support blockwise generation. However, existing estimation schemes randomly sample the block index, so they may evaluate the same block more than once and leave some blocks unevaluated. This introduces variance arising from block selection into the estimates. In this work, we propose a full-block estimator that evaluates every block exactly once and averages the results. We theoretically show that the proposed estimator remains unbiased while eliminating the between-group variance arising from block selection, thereby achieving lower variance than the baseline for the same sample size. In experiments on five benchmarks spanning mathematics, planning, and coding, our method achieves the highest accuracy on four benchmarks and outperforms the random block sampling baseline by an average of 12.79 points.

Does the Ranking Hold? A Practitioner-Facing Audit of Hallucination Detectors on Small, Quantized, On-Device LLMs
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.43)

作者：Shubhra Mittal, Gaurav Naresh Mittal

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.17339v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Hallucination detection for large language models is dominated by methods evaluated on 7B-70B research models with abundant sampling budgets and exact-match or ROUGE correctness labels. The small models practitioners actually ship on-device, Phi-3-mini, Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, and Gemma-2-2B-Instruct, typically at 4-bit precision, sit outside this evidence base. We present a reproducible one-GPU audit of four detectors spanning three paradigms: length-normalized token-margin calibration, a lexical n-gram baseline, and two sampling-based consistency methods (semantic entropy and SelfCheckGPT-NLI) on two small open instruction-tuned models (Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct and Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct) loaded at 4-bit NF4 quantization on a single Colab T4 GPU, evaluated on closed-book TriviaQA. Cross-model ranking transfer is poor: Spearman rho is -0.80 and Kendall tau-b is -0.667, meaning the detector ranking actively inverts between the two models, and the cheap lexical baseline is statistically indistinguishable from the sampling-based consistency detectors on both models under bootstrap 95 percent confidence intervals. As a second, methodological contribution, we show that the standard token-overlap-to-multiple-choice correctness labeler for TruthfulQA-Generation produces near-chance AUROC (0.46-0.59) for all four detectors on both models, a labeler failure, not a detector failure, since the same detectors reach 0.69-0.89 AUROC on TriviaQA under the identical pipeline. We release the harness, prompts, and per-item scores and frame the detector choice explicitly as a fairness-of-error-rates allocation problem for practitioners deploying on resource-constrained, on-device hardware.

For RQ2, an error decomposition attributes the error increase to oversized revisions, and a training-free rule that limits them outperforms forecasts both with and without text for the four models with this trade-off, lowering MASE by 4.49%-21.98% and raising DA by 2.99%-9.71%.
