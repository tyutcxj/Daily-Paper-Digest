EgoExplorer: A Vision-Motion-Touch Observatory for Human Loco-Manipulation In Situ
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.74)

作者：Yunsong Zhou, Xuekun Jiang, Haiqi Shen, Hangxu Liu, Chaojian Shi, Yujie Liu, Jingyuan Li, Haoran Yang, Jiafei Cao, Dongzhan Zhou, Dong Wang, Yao Mu, Chunhua Shen, Weinan Zhang

发布日期：2026-10-13T17:59:55Z

类别：cs.CV

摘要

Learning humanoid loco-manipulation from human demonstrations rests on observing how locomotion, posture, and object interaction evolve together as tasks unfold. Scaling collection across diverse environments, in turn, favors lightweight, portable capture systems. Yet, existing datasets provide only partial views of this coordination. Thus, we introduce EgoExplorer, a 100-hour vision-motion-touch observatory of human loco-manipulation built with a wearable system. With an emphasis on professional activities, its continuous recordings align egocentric video with reconstructed whole-body motion, fine-grained finger articulation, and tactile observations, accompanied by step-level descriptions. To achieve this coverage with sparse body sensing, we develop the Calibrate-Complete-Constrain pipeline. Concretely, reusable calibration fixes participant geometry and sensor attachments before motion inference to disentangle mounting offsets from articulation. A temporal prior then completes unobserved joint motion, while layered constraints promote measurement agreement, temporal continuity, and geometric consistency, properties that motion plausibility alone does not guarantee. Against tracker-fitted references, the method reduces body-point error by 71.9% relative to the strongest baseline; matched evaluations also show gains in hand stability and contact estimation. Preliminary studies explore multimodal generation and conditioning alongside humanoid retargeting with whole-body control in simulation.

AI 总结

研究问题：人类全身协调操作观察数据不足，现有数据集仅提供部分视角。 主要方法：构建EgoExplorer可穿戴观测系统，开发Calibrate-Complete-Constrain流水线处理稀疏传感数据。 关键贡献：提供100小时连续多模态数据集，身体点误差比最强基线降低71.9%。

Visual Tokens on Demand: Visual Budgeting for Scalable and Efficient Multi-Image Reasoning in Multimodal LLMs
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.57)

作者：Aimon Rahman, Joymallya Chakraborty, Vidit Bansal, Abhishek Dan

发布日期：2026-10-13T17:59:24Z

类别：cs.CV

摘要

Multimodal large language models have advanced rapidly, but most remain optimized for single-image inputs. Multi-image reasoning is costly because each image adds many visual tokens, increasing encoding and attention demands. Existing techniques that address the above issue, including visual token pruning, token merging, visual token compression, learned resampling, hierarchical compression, retrieval-augmented visual reasoning, and memory-based architectures, have largely been designed for single-image scenarios, where redundancy is primarily spatial and local. Multi-image reasoning introduces additional challenges: redundancy may occur across images, task-relevant evidence may be sparse or distributed, and aggressive per-image compression can remove details needed for comparison, counting, temporal reasoning, retrieval, or evidence grounding. Moreover, existing token-reduction methods are often either (i) training-free but inference-inefficient, typically relying on accessing attention matrices to get token-importance that may be impractical to access with memory-efficient attention implementations such as FlashAttention, or (ii) training-dependent, requiring the base MLLM to be retrained or adapted to a new compressed-token paradigm. In this work, we study scalable multi-image reasoning and understanding without retraining the base model and without accessing attention matrices at inference time. To this end, we propose mmVB, a lightweight plug-and-play module that assigns query-conditioned per-image token budgets via cross-image context, adding only 1.6M trainable parameters while reducing visual input tokens by 9x. This reduction yields 5x faster time-to-first-token and 7x fewer prefill FLOPs, while maintaining 94.4% of full-resolution accuracy across eight multi-image reasoning benchmarks.

研究问题：多图像推理中视觉令牌冗余导致的高计算成本问题 主要方法：提出视觉预算（Visual Budgeting）方法，按需动态分配视觉令牌 关键贡献：实现可扩展且高效的多图像推理，无需依赖注意力矩阵或重新训练模型

SEAR: Self-Evolving Agentic Robotics
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.50)

作者：Xinyi Wang, Bangzheng Li, Hanyu Wang, Lin Zhang, Andrea Yaoyun Cui, Zhenggang Tang, Shyamal Buch, Dejia Xu

发布日期：2026-10-13T17:40:20Z

类别：cs.AI

摘要

Frontier language-model agents can now improve robots on their own: they write control code, test it, and keep what works as skills and memories for the next task. We introduce SEAR (Self-Evolving Agentic Robotics), a framework and benchmark built around two questions. Is the agent really learning, or just trying more often? And does what it learns still help on new tasks, in a new simulator, and on a real robot? The framework runs an agent in three modes: it writes code that becomes the policy (code-as-policy), acts live in the control loop (agent-as-policy), or works as a researcher that fine-tunes a vision-language-action model (auto-research). The benchmark adds controls that separate retained experience from repeated attempts, familiar tasks, privileged simulator state, larger budgets, and luck. Across seven frontier language models, 330 simulated and five physical tasks, and over four years of agent runtime, we highlight three findings. First, the learning curve of a robotic agent depends both on the overall time budget and the task difficulty relative to model capability. Harder tasks make longer sessions worthwhile; on easier tasks, re-rolling short attempts does just as well. Second, control code and shared routines transfer across tasks and simulators, while task-specific solutions do not. On a real robot, we observe a performance gap between agent-designed practice and novel human-designed scenes. Third, different models evolve more effectively in different ways. Some do best acting live, others writing code first. And a text-only model keeps pace with a comparable multimodal one: perception helps with error assessment, but agentic reasoning and tool use can matter more. We release the benchmark and codebase to support future research.

AI 总结

研究问题：机器人代理是否真正学习，所学内容能否迁移到新任务、新模拟器和真实机器人？ 主要方法：提出SEAR框架，包含三种模式（代码即策略、代理即策略、自动研究）和带控制变量的基准测试。 关键贡献：发现学习曲线依赖时间预算和任务难度；控制代码可跨任务迁移，任务特定方案不能；真实机器人存在性能差距。

发布日期：2026-10-13T17:58:41Z

类别：cs.AI

摘要

Multi-objective molecular optimization is a critical process in drug discovery that aims to modify a given molecule to simultaneously improve multiple properties while maintaining a similar overall structure. LLM ensemble gains are often measured against a single baseline, but the value attributed to additional candidates can change with the comparison used. We study an LLM aggregator under four matched conditions: Direct (no candidates), Single-slot (one candidate in a multi-slot interface), Duplicate (one candidate repeated), and Multi-candidate (separately generated candidates).
