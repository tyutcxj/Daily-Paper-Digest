Video-as-Experience: Learning Computer Use via Audio-Visual Understanding
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.53)

作者：Guangzhi Sun, Zhonghao Zhai, Yixuan Li, Chao Zhang

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.18747v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multimodal large language models (LLMs) have enabled capable computer-use agents that perceive graphical user interfaces, reason over instructions, and execute actions. However, existing approaches to learning from video demonstrations typically convert videos into screenshot-based action trajectories, rather than directly leveraging their rich audio-visual information. We introduce VEX (Video as EXperience), a framework that uses audio-visual understanding to unify learning from external demonstrations and reflecting on an agent's own behavior. Following a watch-act-rewatch paradigm, the agent extracts procedural guidance from tutorial videos containing visual demonstrations and spoken explanations, executes tasks, and verifies its progress by rewatching its execution as video. We instantiate VEX with video-SALMONN-VEX, a 9B-scale audio-visual computer-use agent built on the video-SALMONN 2 architecture with a Qwen3.5-9B backbone. We further introduce Action-Grounded Guide Optimization (AGGO), a reinforcement learning method that optimizes video-derived guidance for downstream action prediction, and augment AgentNet, OSWorld, and CUA-Gym with over 24K real-world tutorial videos. Experiments on OSWorld and a complementary subset of CUA-Gym demonstrate consistent improvements, with video-SALMONN-VEX achieving a 22% relative increase in task success over its Qwen3.5 backbone on OSWorld. Moreover, its generated guidance improves Gemini-3.5-flash by 10% relatively on OSWorld. These results highlight the potential of audio-visual understanding as a unified interface for procedural learning and self-verification in computer-use agents.

研究问题：现有方法将视频转为截图轨迹，未充分利用音视频信息。 主要方法：提出VEX框架，采用watch-act-rewatch范式，结合音视频理解，并用AGGO强化学习优化。 关键贡献：构建视频-SALMONN-VEX模型，引入24K真实教程视频，在OSWorld等任务上取得一致改进。

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.18866v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In cinematic storytelling, sound and visual transitions do not always coincide in lockstep: dialogue frequently bridges across cuts to reveal reactions or introduces new scenes via J- and L-cuts, while deliberate silences pace the scene. Existing audio-visual generative frameworks, however, lack explicit mechanisms to coordinate independent visual and acoustic timing over extended horizons. As narrative duration and shot complexity scale, these systems struggle to maintain long-range dialogue pacing, often hallucinating speech during intended silences or erroneously driving mouth movements on silent listeners. To enable fine-grained cinematic control, we present DECOUPAGE, a screenplay-driven framework for long-form, multi-shot, and multi-character joint audio-video generation. Rather than coupling modality boundaries by default, DECOUPAGE uncouples generation into independently bounded visual and acoustic tracks governed by persistent character identities. DECOUPAGE builds on a joint audio-video diffusion model with dual-schedule temporal cross-attention, incorporating a directorial Lip-sync mechanism that selectively decouples speech conditioning from visual facial dynamics during off-screen dialogue. On multi-shot conversational benchmarks, DECOUPAGE achieves precise control over shot transitions, audio timing, and visibility-aware lip synchronization, directing 15- to 60-second screenplay-driven audio-video generation with multimodal coherence.

作者：Maryam Shoaeinaeini, Brent Harrison

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2611.18597v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Long-horizon reinforcement learning under sparse and delayed feedback remains an open challenge. Existing return-decomposition methods redistribute episodic rewards to ease credit assignment, but they operate on raw state--action transitions and disregard the subgoal structure that governs meaningful progress in complex tasks. We propose Skill-LR, a hierarchical reinforcement learning framework in which a vision--language model identifies the agent's active subgoal at each timestep and a large language model generates a matching latent reward formulation through a two-stage prompting process. By conditioning reward signals on the currently active subgoal, Skill-LR produces stage-specific credit that evolves with the hierarchical plan, reducing temporal variance and improving sample efficiency. On multi-stage MiniGrid and BabyAI benchmarks, including DoorKey and Unlock-to-Unlock, Skill-LR converges faster and more reliably than sparse environmental rewards and strong credit-assignment baselines such as RUDDER and LaRe, demonstrating that subgoal-aligned latent rewards offer a principled path toward stable, interpretable credit assignment in long-horizon tasks.

Bring Back Language to Actions: Restoring VLM capabilities in VLA Models via Parameter Merging
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.47)

作者：Mohamed Salim Aissi, Olivier Sigaud, Laure Soulier, Nicolas Thome

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.18891v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Vision-Language-Action models (VLAs) are obtained by fine-tuning pre-trained Vision-Language Models (VLMs) on robot demonstration data, enabling action prediction from visual observations and language instructions. However, this fine-tuning induces catastrophic forgetting, substantially degrading the semantic and visual grounding capabilities of the original VLM. We address this by merging the parameters of a VLM and its VLA derivative without retraining either model. Concretely, we extract the task vector (the difference between VLA and VLM weights) and decompose it via Singular Value Decomposition. For each component, we learn a scalar mixing coefficient controlling how much of the VLA adaptation is retained, optimised to jointly preserve action prediction and vision-language capabilities. Experiments show that our merged model performs well on both axes simultaneously, consistently outperforming established merging baselines.

How to make a moral agent: sentimentalism and rationalism as lenses on AI
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.46)

作者：David Gottlieb, Jared Moore

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.18714v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Can AI systems be moral agents? That is, can they undertake actions that can be correctly assessed as morally right or wrong? There is no settled normative criterion of moral agency that applies neatly outside paradigm human cases. Because of the difficulties with a normative account, we instead propose a research program to explore descriptive moral agency. Most basically, we ask, what are the capabilities we humans possess that underlie our moral agency? To do so, we draw on historical scholarship in philosophy and moral psychology to isolate candidate capabilities. Then we suggest refining candidate capabilities so that they are testable in the context of AI systems. Finally, we offer specific empirical recommendations to test AI systems, especially large language models (LLMs), for those refined capabilities. Our approach offers several benefits. Firstly, it may demonstrate whether the behaviors of AI systems are aligned, or in a broad sense desirable. Secondly, the debate may inform policy questions (e.g., what models should be deployed in which contexts?). Thirdly, our approach may complement engineering and design efforts: when capabilities can be precisely characterized, they can inform the development of models. arXiv:2611.18640v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Knowledge graphs constructed from text can contain facts that are individually plausible yet inconsistent with their relational context. We introduce SERAG, a supervised anomaly detector that combines two complementary plausibility estimates: a semantic score computed from separately encoded subject, relation, and object labels, and a relational score obtained through a relational graph convolutional network. The scores are standardized, calibrated, and fused. We evaluate the method on a subset of ICEWS18 with vocabulary-constrained synthetic anomalies generated by an LLM, using pooled and chronological splits. At 10% anomaly prevalence, SERAG achieves test AUPRCs of 0.321 and 0.258, respectively, compared with 0.159 and 0.146 for the strongest adapted baseline. Fusion improves mean test AUPRC over either branch alone, with a larger gain in the pooled protocol and a smaller validation-selected gain in the chronological protocol. Additional anomaly supervision improves performance. These results support the complementarity of textual and relational signals for detecting synthetic anomalies under the evaluated protocols.
