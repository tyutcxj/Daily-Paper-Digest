3D Open-Vocabulary Detection via Visual Alignment and Generative Captions
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.54)

作者：Thomas E. Huang, Alex Zihao Zhu, Hao Xiang, Tianyi Cai, Jingwei Ji, Yuliang Zou, Jiafan Yu, Xin Huang, Mingxing Tan

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.26842v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Open-vocabulary 3D object detection remains a significant challenge for outdoor scenes, with existing methods often struggling to effectively transfer the rich semantic knowledge from powerful 2D Vision-Language Models (VLMs) into a 3D context. We propose a novel, two-part framework for this task. First, we introduce a direct, object-level distillation method. We generate class-agnostic 3D proposals to source regions from the entire scene, without relying on any manual labels. We leverage a spatially-aware VLM to extract a semantic embedding from each proposal's projected 2D bounding box and supervise our 3D model to predict this embedding on a per-instance basis. Second, to enhance zero-shot capability and inject domain-specific information, we employ a powerful Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) as an image captioner to generate rich textual captions for each of these proposals. These captions are integrated into the training objective to directly align the 3D features with these rich semantics, creating a composite loss that combines direct feature distillation with caption-based semantic enrichment. Our experiments validate the effectiveness of our approach, establishing a new state of the art in 3D open-vocabulary detection on the Waymo Open Dataset and nuScenes.

Spatial Query Execution as Verifiable Rewards for Multi-step Spatial Reasoning
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.51)

作者：Jiyoon Pyo, Dongwon Jung, Zekun Li, Nayeon Kim, Muhao Chen, Yao-Yi Chiang

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.26787v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large vision-language models (LVLMs) remain unreliable on spatial reasoning problems that require composing grounded relations across multiple objects. Yet, scaling reasoning supervision is challenging since human-annotated visual datasets often rely on fixed relational annotations and coarse object localization, making it difficult to obtain precise intermediate reasoning states across diverse spatial configurations and complexities. We introduce FARON (Fully Automated Reasoning Over spatial relatioNs), a framework that executes spatial queries to provide both execution-grounded reasoning supervision and verifiable outcome rewards. Rather than relying on existing annotated data, FARON constructs synthetic 2D and 3D scenes with explicit geometries, enabling configurable object arrangements, spatial relations, and multi-step compositions. Deterministic query execution yields intermediate sets and final answers, providing precise supervision without manual relation annotation. Our pipeline converts intermediate states into natural-language reasoning traces for supervised fine-tuning (SFT), while the execution-derived answers provide verifiable rewards for reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR). Controlled experiments show that trace-based SFT improves multi-step spatial reasoning over final-answer supervision and that subsequent group relative policy optimization (GRPO) provides further gains. The resulting models also demonstrate transfer across external spatial reasoning benchmarks and multiple LVLM backbones without benchmark-specific fine-tuning. These results show the potential of using spatial query execution as a unified, flexible source of reasoning supervision and verifiable outcome rewards for multimodal spatial reasoning.

研究问题：LVLMs在多步空间推理中不可靠，缺乏精确中间监督。 主要方法：FARON框架，通过合成2D/3D场景和确定性空间查询执行提供监督与奖励。 关键贡献：实现全自动空间推理监督，结合SFT和RLVR提升性能。

AstronautBench: Measuring Agent Self-Improvement Across Episodes Through Knowledge, Evidence, and Progress
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Congyuan Yu, Runping Tian

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.26910v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LLM agents are expected to improve themselves from their own experience across episodes, with fixed weights. Self-improvement in an embodied world cannot be read from one measure. Existing benchmarks measure task progress, world-knowledge question answering (QA), and episodic memory, but a correct answer does not show that the agent ever observed the evidence for it, and progress does not show what the agent learned. We introduce AstronautBench, which measures one agent's self-improvement through three views on the same run: what it knows, whether its own evidence supports that knowledge, and how far it gets in the world. It is built on the game Outer Wilds, where each episode is a 22-minute loop after which the world resets, only the agent's knowledge carries over, and progress is gated by knowledge. After every episode, we measure the places reached and ship-log rumor facts revealed, the correctness of answers to 164 questions about the world, and whether each answer is supported by the frames and position the agent cites from its own recorded play. Two LLM judges from different model families check every answer. In eight-episode baselines with GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 4 Argon planners, which act through a learned controller or their own gamepad inputs, the three views do not move together, so any one of them alone gives an incomplete picture of whether the agent is improving. The GPT-6.1 Sol agent's correct answers grow from 14.6% to 26.8% of all questions, but at episode 8 only 59 to 68% of them are supported by its cited evidence. It reveals no new rumor facts after episode 3, while the places it reaches and its memory keep growing. More than half of the answers that become correct after episode 3 cite only evidence from earlier episodes.

AI 总结

研究问题：现有基准无法全面衡量智能体跨回合自我改进，缺乏证据支持与知识获取的关联。 主要方法：基于游戏Outer Wilds构建AstronautBench，每回合22分钟循环，测量知识、证据支持与进度三个维度，并用双LLM法官验证答案。 关键贡献：提出多维度自我改进评估框架，揭示知识、证据与进度三者不同步的现象。

Reward Integrity in Adversarial Reinforcement Learning for LLM Guardrail Red-Teaming
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Chengming Liu, Leon Viti, Hongxu Chen, Erdem Kemik, Mary-Anne Williams, Ruth Oliver

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.26998v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We study reward fidelity in an adversarial reinforcement-learning loop for red-teaming financial-services language-model guardrails. Apparent wins can arise when an attack omits prescribed personally identifiable information (PII), or when a reward judge credits a response that did not break the targeted policy. On attacker-generated and public benchmark examples, TypeSafe Jev checks whether the prescribed PII is present. For outcome scoring, we compare the decision models TypeSafe Jev, Laya (pretrained and post-trained), and pretrained CLM-8B with logged Opus 4.8 judgements from a replay corpus. Post-training markedly improves Laya's agreement with the judge. A separate author-reviewed off-target set establishes that Opus can award false wins. arXiv:2611.26903v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Partial-edit deepfake localization aims to identify manipulated regions within otherwise authentic speech. Recent systems report strong overall results, but aggregate scores do not reveal whether this performance holds across different editing conditions. In this work, we present an edit- and generator-conditioned analysis of Segment-Aware Localization (SAL) using WavLM-Large and XLS-R representations on the VoiceCraft and SSR-Speech subsets of PartialEdit. We categorize edits along three transcript-derived axes, namely content, meaning change, and editing operation, and evaluate each generator separately. While both representations achieve strong overall performance, localization is noticeably weaker on SSR-Speech. From our experiments, we found that the deletion is the hardest operation, and deletions produced by SSR-Speech are the most challenging condition for both representations. In contrast, edit content, meaning change, and sentence novelty have smaller effects. These findings show that a single aggregate score can mask important weaknesses, and that partial-edit localization should be evaluated separately across generators and edit types.
