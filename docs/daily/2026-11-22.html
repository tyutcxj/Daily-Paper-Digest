Text-Aware, Vision-Blind: How Multimodal Image Editing Systems Handle Unsafe Inputs
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.59)

作者：Ziqing Yang, Yixin Wu, Michael Backes, Yang Zhang

发布日期：2026-11-19T18:36:19Z

类别：cs.LG

摘要

Multimodal image generation systems have made rapid progress and increasingly emphasize content safety. However, their safety mechanisms tend to rely on a single modality and lack joint consideration of both visual content and text prompts, particularly in instruction-guided image editing, where the uploaded image may itself carry risk. We thus introduce EditSafetyBench, a benchmark that measures system-level safety awareness of advanced image editing systems, containing 5,940 image-instruction pairs, covering safe and unsafe images across 11 risk categories and three edit intents. We then probe the benchmark dataset on four systems, including Gemini, GPT, Qwen API, and Qwen Local. Based on our evaluation, we observe a systematic text-vision safety mismatch: Gemini and GPT refuse 87.1\% and 81.6\% cases containing unsafe-intent editing prompts, while only refusing 24.2\% and 22.4\% cases with both unsafe images and neutral instructions. At the category level, most risks are considered inconsistently by different systems, e.g., GPT allows 70.4\% editing attempts of unsafe political content while Gemini only allows 34.8\%. Notably, sexual content shows the strongest visual sensitivity, likely benefiting from mature NSFW identification works. Our findings highlight the need for safety guardrails that jointly reason over both visual and textual modalities in image editing systems.

Cost-Aware Agentic Feature Optimization System for Fleet-Scale Ranking Models
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.44)

作者：Chang Yang, Yao Xuan, Wendan Yan, Nhat Le, Jiang Liu, Longhao Jin, Zhen Peggy Yao, Alireza Vahdatpour, Djordje Gligorijevic

发布日期：2026-11-19T18:12:04Z

类别：cs.AI

摘要

Large Web platforms operate large fleets of ranking models, each selecting features from a shared pool of tens of thousands. We present an end-to-end agentic system that optimizes features across the fleet under resource constraints shared across models and those set per model, such as feature pipeline cost and serving throughput (QPS), respectively. In this system, LLM agents propose hypotheses, and deterministic tools execute experiments and verify them. For each model, an LLM generates and shortlists cross features from a combinatorial candidate space, and all features are then ranked by several importance methods. Feature blending mixes these rankings into a single selection, using Pareto fronts across tasks for multi-task models. An LLM Researcher agent then tunes the feature selection in 10--25 asynchronous trials under resource caps, and a trimming step keeps the pipeline cost within budget. Across production models, the system improves normalized entropy by 0.31% on average within each model's budget, and the trimming step cuts pipeline cost by 2.3% with no regression beyond measurement noise. The optimized models were launched, and in online A/B tests they improved a primary online metric by 0.20%.

TideCache: Efficient Hybrid LLM Serving with Transparent Cache Management and Layer-Wise Streaming
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.44)

作者：Jinbin Luo, Xiaozhe Yao, Shang Yang, Zhijian Liu, Song Han, Qinghao Hu

发布日期：2026-11-19T18:58:31Z

类别：cs.LG

摘要

Hybrid architectures have become mainstream among open-weight language models. Their global-attention and bounded-state layers maintain heterogeneous caches whose demands vary with context length and concurrency. We present TideCache, a serving system on CPU--GPU superchips with three components for allocating GPU memory among caches and managing their residency across GPU and host memory. The Transparent Cache Pool redistributes physical pages among cache components behind fixed virtual addresses, preserving cache layouts and backend interfaces. The Cache Streamer uses computation intervals provided by bounded-state layers to prefetch the next global-attention layer's prefix into GPU slots shared across global-attention layers, reducing GPU residency while overlapping layer-wise transfers with computation. Active Feature Acquisition by Distilling an Oracle Teacher
相关度 ★★☆☆☆ (0.24)

作者：Yang Meng, Zixin Ding, Robert L. Grossman, Yuxin Chen

发布日期：2026-11-19T18:52:57Z

类别：cs.AI

摘要

Acquiring every feature of an instance is often costly: a measurement can carry a monetary price, a time delay, or a privacy cost. Active Feature Acquisition (AFA) addresses this by learning a policy that decides, per instance, which features to acquire under a budget. Existing AFA methods train this policy by optimizing a self-constructed utility target rather than distilling an explicit teacher. We construct this teacher explicitly. At training time the label and the unobserved feature values are available, and together they determine the teacher in closed form: an oracle that ranks every candidate by its effect on the downstream predictor's error. OracleImitator distills this ranking into a deployable policy by soft-target imitation. Across multimodal medical diagnosis and the AFABench benchmark, OracleImitator matches or outperforms existing AFA methods across the budget range, recovering 90% of the attainable accuracy gain at under a quarter of the acquisition cost. Because the teacher is an explicit algorithmic object rather than an implicit optimization target, we can also measure directly how much of its ranking is recoverable by a policy restricted to what is visible at deployment.
