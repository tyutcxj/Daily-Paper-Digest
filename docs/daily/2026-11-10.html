Turkish Multimodal Video Fact-Checking: A New Dataset and a Comparison of Zero-Shot Agentic and Fine-Tuned Systems
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.62)

作者：Azra A\c{c}{\i}l

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.08651v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Video-based misinformation spreads rapidly on social media, but almost all existing fact-checking systems focus exclusively on English text. Tools for low-resource languages such as Turkish are severely limited. In this work, we present TeViD (Turkish Video Fact-checking Dataset), to the best of our knowledge the first multimodal video fact-checking dataset for Turkish. We compiled the dataset from articles published by Teyit.org, an IFCN-certified organisation, and enriched it with translated Snopes articles and synthetic examples to reduce class imbalance. Building on TeViD, we develop two verification pipelines that share the same open-source base model. First, in 3MFact-TR (Pipeline 1), we adapt the multi-role 3MFact framework to Turkish with the Qwen2.5-7B large language model and the Qwen2-VL-7B vision-language model, and add three modules of our own: a semantic inconsistency checker, a 5W1H question manager, and a bilingual evidence retriever that excludes fact-checking sites to prevent leakage. Second, as our Fine-Tuned Model (Pipeline 2), we fine-tune a single Qwen2.5-7B with Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) to produce closed-book verdicts from the claim and the video's textual description. We report accuracy, macro F1, True class F1, and True class recall for both systems, together with ablation studies.

Debate to Deceive: Poisoning LLM's Fact-Checking via Game-Theoretic Multi-Agent Debate
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.55)

作者：Liting Huang, Huizhi Liang, Yi Lei, Xingjun Ma, Varun Ojha

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.08756v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Knowledge attacks on Large Language Models, such as manipulating fact-checking judgments, pose significant risks to AI reliability. Existing approaches -- adversarial suffix/template injection and single-agent self-reflection -- lack robustness across diverse defenses and are prone to confirmation bias and costly trial-and-error, respectively. We propose Debate-to-Deceive (D2D), a multi-agent debate framework grounded in human debate structure and rhetorical style, enabling agents to generate adversarial passages that are semantically coherent, rhetorically effective, and resistant to early confirmation bias. D2D formalizes the adversarial process as an evolutionary game in which agents adaptively evolve their rhetorical strategies (Logos, Pathos, Ethos) through competition. To manage the intractably large LLM output space, we decouple agent behavior into a strategic layer, which selects a rhetorical action from a compact strategy space via evolutionary-inspired learning dynamics, and a generation layer, where the LLM produces a concrete passage conditioned on that strategy and the debate context. On four fact-checking benchmarks (FEVER, HealthVer, CLIMATE-FEVER, and SciFact), D2D substantially outperforms existing attacks and evades most defenses.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何通过多智能体辩论生成对抗性文本以欺骗LLM事实核查。 主要方法：提出D2D框架，将对抗过程形式化为进化博弈，智能体竞争演化修辞策略，分策略层和生成层。 关键贡献：在四个基准上显著优于现有攻击，能逃避多数防御。

Memory-Aware Multi-Agent Architecture with LLM-based Orchestration for Conversational Recommendation

作者：Emilly Pitman de Castro, Vanessa Silva, Gustavo Kanno, Marcus Santos, Bruno Ferraz do Amaral, Marcelo Nogueira, Lucas Francisco Pellicer, Lucas Lanaro, Pedro Augusto Luiz, Jo\~ao Renato Ribeiro Manesco, Giovanni Carbinatti, Isabela de Lima Santos, Lucas Oliveira Jeronymo, Jeison Fappi, Raul Rotilli Aguirre, Fabio Andr\'e Frantz, Renata De Paris, Fabiana Marucci, Lohan Silva, Vinicius Diniz Reolon, Wandemberg Gibaut

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.08718v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Conversational recommender systems often treat user sessions in isolation, limiting their ability to capture how user intent evolves over time. While large language models (LLMs) have improved semantic understanding, they are typically used as isolated components, without addressing how memory, retrieval, and decision-making should be integrated at the system level. In this work, we propose a memory-aware multi-agent architecture for conversational recommendation, in which LLMs act as orchestration layers that coordinate intent refinement, query construction, and interaction flow. A key design principle is the integration of long-term memory as a first-class component, influencing both routing decisions and semantic query formation. We evaluate the system through cross-session memory experiments, multi-turn conversational simulations, and supervisor-driven interaction studies on the Amazon Reviews dataset. Results show that memory-enabled interactions recover up to 63\% of the gap toward explicit-query scenarios and prevent semantic degradation in extended dialogues. In addition, supervisory orchestration increases user acceptance by +28.9 percentage points, highlighting the importance of structured orchestration in guiding interaction and output quality. We also observe an exposure bottleneck between retrieved candidates and displayed recommendations. While LLM-reranking does not improve Hit@K, it consistently enhances semantic consistency and diversity. To capture these effects, we introduce the Semantic Consistency Score (SCS), a complementary evaluation measure. Overall, the results emphasize the importance of system-level design choices, suggesting that effective conversational recommendation benefits from explicit memory integration, orchestration, and evaluation beyond traditional ranking metrics.

作者：Nima Vahdat, Pashootan Vaezipoor

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.08644v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LLM agents do not learn from corrections across sessions. A user fixes the same mistake at the start of every conversation. Existing agent memory systems primarily handle facts, such as preferences, entities, and conversational history, rather than procedural knowledge. We present Forge Decision Context (forge_dc), a system that captures behavioral signals from human corrections and self-assessed tool-call failures, compiles them into imperative RULE directives, and injects them at the decision points where they apply. We evaluate forge_dc alongside four predecessor approaches on DC-Bench, a new benchmark of 71 scenarios across nine behavioral dimensions, built from real executed agent traces in a deterministic mocked-tool environment. forge_dc reaches 95% compliance, up from 55% with naive retrieval, while injecting approximately 10 times fewer tokens per query. The results identify representation, activation, and placement as central design choices for persistent behavioral learning.

arXiv:2611.08755v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have achieved strong performance on static visual understanding benchmarks, yet their ability to act in dynamic environments remains less well understood. We introduce AtariBench, a controlled benchmark that evaluates VLMs across 20 Atari 2600 games, requiring models to make sequential decisions from high-temporal-resolution gameplay streams. Aspect-wise Pairwise Tournaments for Test-Time Selection
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.45)

作者：Hannah Guan, Yilun Du

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.08640v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Scaling test-time compute has proven highly effective at improving LLM reasoning. A popular method is best-of-n sampling, where a model generates many candidate solutions and one is selected. This motivates the question: how can we select the best candidate? When humans make choices, we find it more natural to compare two options than judge each in isolation. Additionally, when the options become complex, we like to judge the options one aspect at a time, such as comparing two job candidates on experience, communication, etc. In the test-time setting, candidate solutions should be judged in the same way. Here we introduce Aspect-wise Pairwise Tournaments (APT), which verifies by comparison and compares by aspect. Each matchup of a single elimination tournament is decided by a vote over multiple off-the-shelf aspect verifiers, each expressing a pairwise preference along its own aspect. Across five domains and three generator LLMs, APT improves over binary multi-aspect approval, self-consistency and reward model verification, with the largest gains on open-ended tasks. We find that both components matter; neither binary approval over the same aspects nor a generic pairwise judge at a matched budget matches APT. Because aspects are specified in natural language, APT also lets users steer selection toward properties they care about, better aligning with user preferences without needing to regenerate solutions. No component is trained, so APT transfers to new domains by simply rewriting prompts.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何在大语言模型测试时选择最佳候选答案？ 主要方法：提出逐方面成对锦标赛（APT），通过多方面验证器投票决定配对胜负。 关键贡献：APT在五个领域和三个生成器上优于基线方法，支持用户按方面偏好选择。

Subliminal Learning in Vision-Language Models
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.45)

作者：Arman Hatami, Romina Aalishah, Ilya E. Monosov

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.08538v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Subliminal learning is a phenomenon in large language models (LLMs) where a teacher passes a hidden preference to a student through training data unrelated to that preference. We study whether subliminal learning also occurs in vision-language models (VLMs). We finetune a teacher VLM to prefer a specific animal or facial attribute and use a control teacher from the same base model. Both teachers generate numbers for an unrelated visual task involving geometric shapes. Two students initialized from the same base VLM are finetuned on the same images, using numbers generated by either the biased or control teacher. Across VLMs ranging from 2B to 7B parameters, we find that both animal and facial preferences can transfer through this unrelated task. Students trained on biased-teacher outputs select the target more often than control students, with a difference of up to $\sim$10 percentage points in the animal preference task. Transfer can become stronger with more training data and varies with the target preference, model, and how the numbers are generated. Beyond preferences, we show that the biased student's explanations use more of the same words and phrases as the biased teacher's explanations than those of the control student, even when all three models select the same image. These results matter for training VLMs and show that training on unrelated data may change both a VLM's image preferences and how it explains its choices, and that transfer can become stronger with more training samples.

研究问题：视觉语言模型中是否存在通过无关训练数据传递隐藏偏好的潜意识学习。 主要方法：微调教师VLM使其偏好特定属性，再为无关几何任务生成数字，学生基于这些数字微调并测试偏好传递。 关键贡献：发现动物和面部偏好可通过无关任务传递，差异达10个百分点，且更多数据增强传递，学生解释与教师更相似。

摘要

arXiv:2611.08613v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Normative competence, the ability to infer socially enforced expectations from interaction and adjust behavior accordingly, is essential for agents operating in unfamiliar human communities. Yet existing evaluations largely test whether agents can recognize or apply norms they may already know, rather than whether they can acquire previously unknown norms from social interaction. We introduce NormGym, an interactive benchmark for normative competence in which a newcomer must infer a hidden, arbitrary norm while pursuing its own task in a multi-agent community. Success requires distinguishing what is possible from what is permitted, inferring the norm from observed behavior and sanctions, and applying it in new situations. NormGym contains diverse norms and distinct environments, allowing systematic evaluation of current agents, including large language model (LLM) agents. We find that normative competence remains challenging: the strongest evaluated LLM achieves a joint task-and-compliance success rate of 55.3%. Our analysis reveals that models can sometimes revise their behavior following sanctions for their own actions, but struggle to use other agents' behavior and the sanctions they receive to infer the underlying norm. We further find that fine-tuning models may be insufficient to improve normative competence. These results suggest that current models lack a robust, transferable capability for acquiring normative knowledge through social interaction.

摘要

arXiv:2611.08669v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Speculative decoding has become a standard way to accelerate LLM inference: a lightweight drafter proposes tokens that the target verifies in one parallel pass. Existing drafters face trade-offs that limits their speedups: autoregressive drafters generate tokens one at a time, so drafting cost grows with draft length, while diffusion drafters generate tokens in parallel but predict each position independently, ignoring inter-token dependencies. We introduce ChainFlow, a new speculative decoding paradigm and the first flow-matching drafter conditioned on the target's hidden states. ChainFlow treats drafting as a short transport over hidden states, needing one or two flow steps instead of the many denoising steps of generation from noise. A variational autoencoder compresses these states so drafting cost barely grows with target size, lightweight feedback heads restore inter-token dependencies, and tree drafting raises acceptance. On Qwen3.5 models, at matched drafter size and training data, ChainFlow outperforms EAGLE-3 and DFlash in vLLM by 39% and 11%, respectively. These gains further widen as drafters are adapted to new languages sequentially. Our ablations cover drafter design and concurrent serving, and we open-source our code and drafter models.

AI 总结

研究问题：现有投机解码草稿模型存在自回归成本高或忽略token间依赖的权衡。 主要方法：提出ChainFlow，首个基于流匹配的草稿模型，以目标隐藏状态为条件，用1-2步流步进行短传输，结合VAE压缩和轻量反馈头。 关键贡献：在Qwen3.5上，相同规模下比EAGLE-3和DFlash分别提升39%和11%，且在新语言上增益扩大。

摘要

arXiv:2611.08752v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models have achieved remarkable performance across a wide range of tasks, yet they remain susceptible to jailbreak attacks that circumvent safety mechanisms and elicit harmful or unintended outputs. Although safety can be improved through model fine-tuning, such approaches can be computationally expensive and may degrade previously acquired capabilities through catastrophic forgetting. Recent jailbreak defenses have therefore explored targeted interventions on specific model components associated with unsafe behavior. However, existing approaches typically decouple two inherently related decisions: where to intervene and how to modify the selected components. Such sequential optimization may be suboptimal because the optimal intervention location can depend on the intervention applied there, and vice versa. In this work, we introduce AttentionSwitch, the first jailbreak defense to jointly optimize intervention sites and strengths within a frozen LLM. Specifically, AttentionSwitch learns a sparse set of attention heads together with query-conditioned multiplicative scaling factors, allowing the outputs of the selected heads to be either attenuated or amplified without altering the directions of their representations. Across popular language models and jailbreak attacks, AttentionSwitch outperforms existing defenses, with particularly strong results when training data is scarce.

研究问题：LLM易受越狱攻击，现有防御方法将干预位置与强度分开优化导致次优。 主要方法：提出AttentionSwitch，在冻结LLM中联合学习稀疏注意力头选择与查询条件缩放因子。 关键贡献：首次实现干预位置和强度的联合优化，有效防御越狱攻击且不改变表示方向。

Cancellation Is Not Enough: What Retries Leave Behind in an Overloaded LLM Engine
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.41)

作者：Xindan Zhang, Fei Wang

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.08684v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Clients and proxies in front of an LLM engine retry calls that time out. After a transient overload, these retries can keep the engine from recovering even when calls arrive below its capacity. Since the engine may keep computing for clients that have left, propagating cancellation looks like the natural remedy. In this paper, we test it on vLLM with overload experiments on three open models, and with scheduler traces of one that follow each client attempt to the execution it creates in the engine. We find that with retries on, cancellation alone did not restore service in any run, and only 12-38% of the calls that arrived after the overload were delivered. The traces suggest why: retries that vLLM ran typically started prefill less than half a second before they timed out. Executions that vLLM later aborted took 79-85% of the prompt tokens it computed, and vLLM's prompt-token counter missed a third to half of all computed prompt tokens. A retry budget restored service by shedding retries but left many of the overload's calls undelivered. Request coalescing delivered every call from the overload onward, some late, unless cancellation had already aborted the running execution. Overload controls should thus be judged by the executions that retries add and the calls that clients receive.

AI 总结

研究问题：重试请求在LLM引擎过载后阻碍恢复，取消机制是否足够？ 主要方法：在vLLM上对三个开放模型进行过载实验，并跟踪调度器轨迹。 关键贡献：发现取消机制无法恢复服务，重试导致大量未完成计算；提出重试预算和请求合并方法，后者能交付所有请求。
