M-Plan: Comprehensive Planning For LLM Agents with Memory Arrangement
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.61)

作者：Ziyang Liu, Jiakang Yuan, Jiayuan Fan, Tao Chen

发布日期：Wed,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.27032v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language model (LLM)-based agents have recently demonstrated outstanding performance in single-turn tasks, yet they continue to struggle with long-horizon tasks, particularly in environments without prior training. A key limitation of single LLMs is their difficulty in processing long contexts, which stems from weaknesses in continuous decision-making and reasoning capabilities. Although existing multi-agent systems (MAS) equipped with long-term memory bases can partially address this gap, they mostly rely on knowledge reuse or summarization, an approach that is often constrained by directly retrieval without any adaptation for the current task, while the retrieved memory may contain much history noises waiting to be preprocessed. In contrast, MAS characterized by task-specific guidance often lack long-term memory specifically designed for the guidance. To address these challenges, we propose M-Plan, a closed-loop MAS framework that integrates long-term and short-term memory. By combining global-local and past-current guidance, M-Plan enhances agents' performance on multi-turn tasks while maintaining a plug-and-play design. During task execution, agents receive task-specific milestones and insights, and are provided with local hints only when confusion arises, which form global-local guidance together. These guidance generation and utilization construct the short-term memory as well. After task completion, relevant information about the current task is optimized or reorganized before being sent to the long-term memory storage for future retrieval. The designments mentioned above build a comprehensive framework different from other individual long-term or short-term memory works. Extensive experiments on multi-turn tasks including ALFWorld, Webshop, and PDDL across diverse models demonstrate that M-Plan outperforms multiple baseline methods in both accuracy and efficiency.

AI 总结

研究问题：LLM agents在长时任务中处理长上下文和连续决策能力不足。 主要方法：提出M-Plan闭环多智能体框架，整合长期和短期记忆，结合全局-局部与过去-当前指导。 关键贡献：通过记忆安排和即插即用设计，提升多轮任务性能，减少历史噪声干扰。

miniTE: Diagnosing and Bridging the Execution Gap in Native NVFP4 Reinforcement Learning
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.53)

作者：Zhen Li, Mingfa Feng, Yiming Zhang, Yang Yu, Changhai Zhou, Pengxiang Li, Dongkuan Wu, Congkai Xie, Hongxia Yang

发布日期：Wed,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2610.23928v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Native NVFP4 promises to accelerate reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models (LLMs), but achieving stable learning across separate training and sampling engines remains challenging. We show that matching model weights and numerical formats is insufficient to ensure consistent policy evaluation, highlighting the importance of cross-engine numerical execution. We introduce miniTE, a lightweight framework that combines execution diagnostics, cross-engine numerical alignment, and efficient native W4A4 (4-bit weights and activations) GEMM computation for LLM RL. It improves numerical execution consistency without modifying the RL objective, while reducing implementation overhead to realize the benefits of native low-bit computation. Long-horizon RL experiments on Qwen3-30B-A3B demonstrate stable training and benchmark performance close to BF16, outperforming naive Transformer Engine (TE) NVFP4 in evaluation quality. On single NVIDIA B300 GPU node, the optimized learner reaches 33,540 tokens/s, yielding 1.39x and 1.47x the throughput of BF16 and TE NVFP4, respectively, for complete learner updates. We released the framework, kernels, diagnostics, and training configurations to make native low-bit RL easier.

AI 总结

研究问题：原生NVFP4在LLM强化学习中存在训练与采样引擎间的执行差距，导致策略评估不一致。 主要方法：提出miniTE框架，包括执行诊断、跨引擎数值对齐和高效原生W4A4 GEMM计算。 关键贡献：在不修改RL目标下提升数值执行一致性，实现接近BF16的性能，吞吐量提升1.39x-1.47x。

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2610.23926v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fake accounts continue to undermine online platforms, yet it remains unclear whether multimodal learning offers meaningful improvements over strong metadata baselines or graph-based approaches. In this work, we study bot detection by integrating textual, visual, and account metadata signals on the TwiBot-22 benchmark using a subsample of 30,000 users. We propose a multimodal framework combining pretrained textual (XLM-RoBERTa) and visual (CLIP) embeddings with structured account-level features such as follower counts, posting frequency, and account age, and evaluate multiple modality combinations using CatBoost alongside a Relational Graph Convolutional Network (R-GCN). Results show that metadata alone is highly predictive, while multimodal fusion yields only marginal and largely statistically insignificant gains. The graph-based model underperforms classical variants under the subsampled graph conditions of this study. Overall, our findings suggest that under practical constraints, feature quality and metadata richness matter more than fusion complexity.

摘要

arXiv:2610.26913v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The adoption of Large Language Models for radiology report structuring has been largely dominated by either proprietary cloud-based models or large open models requiring high-end GPUs for deployment, which is a critical bottleneck for clinical adoption in radiology workflows that demand data privacy and offline operation. To address this, we present MedPAO-Assist, an edge-deployable agentic framework that generates structured radiology reports directly from radiologist dictation and voice commands, requiring only $\approx3.3$ GB peak RAM and achieving an average end-to-end latency of $\approx31$ seconds on a standard laptop CPU through an asynchronous, multi-threaded execution pipeline. The system currently supports four major imaging modalities -- Chest X-ray, Chest CT, Abdomen CT, and Brain MRI. arXiv:2610.26964v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) struggle with classical planning, but benchmarks rarely distinguish structural difficulty from the difficulty of interpreting symbolic vocabulary. We examine seven STRIPS domains with BFS-optimal plan lengths. For predicting structural difficulty without solving an instance, the zero-training delete-relaxation heuristic $h_{\mathrm{add}}$ outperforms learned alternatives on held-out domains (mean Spearman $|\rho|=0.931$), including ridge regression ($0.707$), object count ($0.657$), and the episodic retrieval architecture ARC ($0.400$). Hybrid routing between an LLM and a symbolic planner depends more on calibration to the target LLM's outcomes than on the structural score selected. Thus, established symbolic heuristics already estimate structural difficulty well; improving hybrid planning requires modeling LLM behavior sensitive to predicate semantics rather than developing more structural predictors.

摘要

arXiv:2610.26995v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LLM-based agents increasingly rely on reusable skills. However, scaling skill libraries makes efficient and accurate skill use difficult. Full skill loading incurs high token and runtime overhead. In contrast, flat semantic retrieval treats skills as independent text entries, ignoring dependencies, execution order, state transitions, and data flow constraints. We propose SPEM, a lightweight structure-aware procedural memory framework for efficient skill use in LLM-based agents. SPEM maintains Atomic Skill Memory for individual skills and Skill-DAG Memory for reusable compositions derived from successful executions. For a new task, SPEM reuses high-confidence Skill-DAG templates when available. Otherwise, it retrieves atomic skills and invokes Skill-GNN for state- and structure-aware skill selection. Successful executions are written back as new Skill-DAGs, allowing procedural memory to accumulate over time. Experiments on ALFWorld and SkillsBench show that SPEM achieves the best overall effectiveness--efficiency trade-off, reaching 87.1% ALFWorld success and 38.38% SkillsBench reward while reducing token and runtime costs against strong baselines.

摘要

arXiv:2610.23864v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Adaptive VLM ensembles save computation by stopping early on easy images and escalating harder ones. Although a single conformal threshold can make the final predictor marginally valid, it can still fail severely along individual computational routes. We characterize this failure precisely: if a route has prevalence pi, marginal 1-alpha coverage allows its conditional coverage to fall to max{0, 1-alpha/pi}. To address this mismatch, we learn the router on development data, freeze all routing choices, and calibrate the returned score separately for early exits and escalated inputs. We call this pipeline SAGE-CP. Its calibration step applies Mondrian split conformal prediction to the operational groups created by the router. Under exchangeability, the resulting prediction sets achieve finite-sample coverage within each route. Across 10 benchmarks, 4 frozen VLMs, and 5 aligned splits, both global calibration and SAGE-CP achieve 0.900 marginal coverage at a 0.90 target, but their route behavior differs substantially. SAGE-CP raises mean worst-route coverage from 0.623 to 0.887 and reduces the mean route-coverage gap from 0.324 to 0.025, using the same routed scores and 1.177 source calls per image. The stronger guarantee increases the mean prediction-set size from 1.893 to 2.212; nevertheless, the sets remain smaller than those of the full ensemble (2.486) while requiring 70.6 percent fewer source calls. The route-level reliability persists across 6 fixed query fractions, while finer exit-depth groups demonstrate the need for additional calibration data on rare routes. Across 5 datasets, SAGE-CP also reduces measured batch-one latency by 58.8 to 63.4 percent and gross GPU energy by 55.7 to 57.5 percent relative to full inference. Thus, routing provides computational savings, while route-stratified calibration attaches coverage guarantees to the operational paths created by the router.
