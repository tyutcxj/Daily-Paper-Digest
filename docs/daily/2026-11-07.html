Terminal-style Mathematical Modeling Bench: The First Agentic and Verifiable Benchmark for Mathematical Modeling
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.58)

作者：Xiaojun Wu, Cehao Yang, Xuhui Jiang, Chengjin Xu, Jia Li, Jian Guo

发布日期：2026-11-05T16:18:35Z

类别：cs.CL

摘要

Mathematical modeling turns an open-ended real-world problem into assumptions, a model, a solution, and a defended recommendation, and its answers matter only if they are both close to a credible reference and physically feasible; existing benchmarks either score solver fragments or grade reports with an LLM judge, leaving those numbers unchecked. We introduce TB-MathModel (the Terminal-style Mathematical Modeling Bench), the first benchmark to make complete contest-style modeling problems agentic, verifiable, and terminal at once, over 18 real CUMCM and MCM problems (2023--2025). Each task ships the verbatim statement, official attachments, and figures in a containerized terminal; the agent returns one JSON artifact, scored on the numeric core of the final question against a reproduction of the Outstanding-award paper. Two design choices distinguish it: direction-aware, one-sided scoring, which never penalises beating the anchor, and two-layer feasibility verification---a programmatic gate over six decidable invariant forms plus 624 Lean~4 obligations---with a hard gate that zeroes infeasible answers. Each task is scored against a panel of human outstanding papers rather than one, so a model must be close to many accepted answers; across seven multimodal entries (three seeds each) the strongest reach a multi-reference O-Eval of 64.58\%, while the \$2.54 open model holds 59.81\% at $1/100$ the cost. TB-MathModel turns the numeric core of modeling into a reproducible, machine-checkable signal for measuring long-horizon modeling competence and for collecting agent trajectories. The source code is available at https://github.com/wxj630/terminal-bench-math-modeling.

AI 总结

研究问题：现有数学建模基准缺乏可验证性和完整性 主要方法：提出TB-MathModel，基于18个真实竞赛问题，容器化终端，JSON输出，方向感知单侧评分和两层可行性验证（程序化门+Lean 4） 关键贡献：首个可验证的端到端数学建模基准，确保答案接近优秀论文且物理可行

FedReason: Large Language Models as Weight-Space Meta-Aggregators for Heterogeneous Federated Learning
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.53)

作者：Ali Mohammadi Ruzbahani, Abbas Yazdinejad, Hadis Karimipour

发布日期：2026-11-05T18:00:58Z

类别：cs.AI

摘要

Federated learning servers combine client models with a fixed aggregation rule, and each rule in common use is designed for one kind of heterogeneity. We study whether a large language model (LLM) that inspects the client models can choose the rule itself, without any training on the server. In FedReason, the LLM is called every ten rounds. It analyses the weights by running code in a restricted server-side sandbox, sees only the printed results, may score a few candidates on a 500-image proxy set, and submits a short Python aggregation program. The server applies the program until the next call and falls back to FedAvg whenever its output is invalid. Full weight tensors never leave the server. We evaluated FedReason with Claude Opus 5.5 on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 with ResNet-18 under Dirichlet label skew, width-heterogeneous sub-models and Byzantine clients, in 168 runs against 14 baselines. Across 12 paired label-skew runs it beat FedAvg by 1.26 percentage points on average (10 wins), and a control that spends the same per-round proxy budget on choosing among standard rules by 1.08 points. It was the strongest server-side rule on CIFAR-100 but did not beat SCAFFOLD, which changes client training. With three of ten clients malicious, it excluded all sign-flipping clients from the first round and stayed about one point below FLTrust without training a root model. Without proxy feedback, accuracy fell from 73.8% to 38.8% in one seed. A 60-round run cost 0.79 USD in API fees on average.

AI 总结

研究问题：异构联邦学习中服务器如何动态选择聚合规则。 主要方法：用LLM每十轮分析客户端权重，生成Python聚合程序，在沙箱中运行。 关键贡献：无需服务器训练，FedReason在标签偏斜等场景下优于FedAvg，能抵御拜占庭攻击。

ProQuant: Progressive Quantization-aware Training for Edge MLLMs
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.51)

作者：Yufei Xue, Yushi Huang, Jiawei Shao, Pingcheng Dong, Yonghao Tan, Shiyao Li, Kwang-Ting Cheng, Jun Zhang

发布日期：2026-11-05T16:04:31Z

类别：cs.CL

摘要

Multi-modal large language models (MLLMs) with impressive perception and understanding capabilities will enable a range of downstream applications. However, their substantial computational and memory requirements hinder the real-world implementation, particularly for resource-constrained edge devices. Quantization has emerged as an effective solution for deploying large models. While post-training quantization (PTQ) successfully retains the performance at moderate bit-width (e.g., W8A8), it suffers from severe performance degradation under low-bit quantization (e.g., W4A8), especially for small-scale models that are more suitable for edge deployment. In this paper, we propose ProQuant, a novel weight-activation quantization-aware training (QAT) framework tailored for low-bit MLLMs. First, to align the limited finetuning dataset distribution with the pretrained dataset distribution, we propose a self-relabeling (SRL) scheme to resample the responses of the open-source multimodal dataset. Second, we are the first to take a decoupled view of multi-modal (MM) understanding and language modeling abilities. We propose a progressive block activation (PBA) mechanism to prioritize the MM understanding recovery. We also introduce a vision-sensitive factor $\vecτ$ that effectively identifies the MM-critical layers. Extensive experiments across various benchmarks, covering models with 2B-8B parameters, show that ProQuant significantly outperforms existing methods. For example, our W4A8 Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct improves average accuracy by impressively 6.6% and achieves performance comparable to full precision counterparts with only approximately 1% performance drop. Code is available at https://github.com/yufeixue-ai/ProQuant/.

研究问题：低比特量化导致边缘MLLM性能严重下降 主要方法：提出ProQuant框架，含自重新标记(SRL)和渐进块激活(PBA)机制 关键贡献：首次解耦多模态理解与语言建模，优先恢复多模态理解能力

However, the process is often slow because the LLM generates a long textual chain of thought for each round and must repeat this generation many times to maintain population diversity. We present SwarmEvolve, a framework in which hundreds of low-cost agents per round work in swarms, reason in latent space without decoding to text, and share information by passing KV caches. Each swarm studies the problem from a different angle. A novelty filter keeps the most distinct agent ideas by directly measuring the distances between the agents' hidden state vectors. Crossover of ideas happens naturally in latent space, as a summarizer agent receives the combined KV caches of all swarm members. On 21 problems in mathematical discovery and scientific computing, SwarmEvolve gets the highest average score and needs a median of only 3 to 8 minutes to reach the best scores that other state-of-the-art frameworks reach in 60 minutes. On several problems, SwarmEvolve with Qwen3 8B improves on the solutions that AlphaEvolve first found with frontier LLMs. Our work shows that agent swarms that communicate in latent space offer a fast, low-cost approach to program search for scientific problems.

AI 总结

研究问题：LLM引导的进化搜索因长文本推理和重复生成导致速度慢。 主要方法：SwarmEvolve框架，数百个智能体在潜在空间推理，通过KV缓存共享信息，用新颖性过滤器保持多样性。 关键贡献：在21个问题上取得最高平均分，仅需3-8分钟达到其他框架60分钟的最佳分数，小模型也能改进前沿方案。

摘要

Much of human culture is tacit and is typically learned through situated social interaction. As LLMs are deployed as interactive agents across diverse communities, assessing their cultural competence demands moving beyond existing question-answering approaches, shifting from what they know to how they adapt. In this work, we broaden the scope of cultural adaptation in NLP by investigating whether LLMs can adapt through repeated interactions via a sandbox environment. By reframing cultural adaptation as a continuous process of norm assimilation through social interaction, our work demonstrates that memory mechanisms foster longitudinal learning across repeated interactions, facilitate transfer to different social settings, and enable multilingual norm assimilation across five languages. Together, our findings lay the groundwork for continuous, interaction-driven cultural adaptation in LLMs.

MedGraph: Maintaining Patient State with Graph Memory for Long-Term Medical Dialogue
相关度 ★★★☆☆ (0.33)

作者：Donghua Zhang, Zizheng Wang, Ze Dong, Yiding Feng, Siyu Qian

发布日期：2026-11-05T18:27:20Z

类别：cs.AI

摘要

LLM-based medical assistants that follow a patient across visits need a memory that keeps the patient's clinical state correct as it changes. We examine the errors of five memory systems on longitudinal medical dialogue and find three recurring causes: the retrieved evidence gives an outdated state, includes another person's health facts, or lacks a safety fact the answer depends on. We present MedGraph, a graph memory built for these three cases. Construction extracts dated facts from each session, attributes each fact to the person it describes, and links medication, diagnosis, and measurement records into chains that keep earlier states. Retrieval ranks the patient's chains as units, reads each chain at the time the question asks about, and adds the original dialogue text; a patient profile lists every recorded safety fact with every question. On MedMemoryBench, MedGraph has the highest overall accuracy with both a GPT and a Qwen backbone, 10.5 and 10.4 points above the strongest of five baselines, with efficient memory construction and answering in the GPT setting.

AI 总结

研究问题：长期医疗对话中维护患者状态正确性，现有记忆系统存在三种错误。 主要方法：提出MedGraph图记忆系统，提取带日期的事实并归属人物，链接药物、诊断、测量记录成链，检索时按链排序并读取特定时间点状态。 关键贡献：在MedMemoryBench上，MedGraph在GPT和Qwen骨干下准确率最高，比最强基线高10.5和10.4分，且构建和回答高效。
