Self-Assessment Reinforcement Learning for Search-State Awareness in Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.53)

作者：Taewhoo Lee, Minju Song, Jungwoo Park, Chanwoong Yoon, Junha Jung, Hyeon Hwang, Sanghoon Lee, Minbyul Jeong, Jaewoo Kang

发布日期：Sat,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2610.30435v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Recent advances in search agents have enabled large language models to tackle complex tasks by retrieving and synthesizing external information. However, existing search agents often lack search-state awareness, i.e., the ability to recognize what information has been obtained and what remains missing, leading to hallucinated reasoning and prematurely terminated search. To address this limitation, we propose Self-Assessment Reinforcement Learning (SARL), a reinforcement learning framework in which models learn to recognize their own search state during training and improve their search behavior accordingly, without requiring additional assessment at test time. SARL achieves this through a dual reward design that captures the corrective effect of each assessment while penalizing excessive reliance on it, jointly encouraging effective and efficient search trajectories. Under this setup, SARL achieves a 24.3% relative improvement over the strongest baseline, with average gains of 35.9% on multi-hop reasoning and 27.9% on deep research benchmarks against the strongest per-benchmark baselines. SARL further surpasses methods built on auxiliary supervision or multi-agent coordination, despite operating as a standalone RL framework with no inference-time scaffolding. Beyond final performance, SARL maintains training stability under extended search horizons, a regime where existing methods consistently degrade, enabling sustained gains as task complexity grows.

AI 总结

研究问题：现有搜索代理缺乏搜索状态意识，导致幻觉推理和过早终止搜索。 主要方法：提出自评估强化学习（SARL），通过双奖励设计让模型在训练中学习识别搜索状态并改进搜索行为。 关键贡献：SARL相对最强基线提升24.3%，多跳推理和深度研究分别提升35.9%和27.9%，超越辅助监督等方法。

作者：Shuo Cai, Yanggan Gu, Yuhang Liu, Su Lu, Yuanyi Wang, Yang Yu, Zhen Li, Zhitian Hou, Guanghao Zhu, Ming Li, Hongxia Yang

发布日期：Sat,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2610.30162v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Model fusion aims to integrate the capabilities of multi-expert models into a single model. Existing evaluations offer limited coverage of reasoning with long chains of thought and tool use, while strong average performance can mask substantial losses in individual expert capabilities. We introduce FusionEval, a benchmark that provides controlled expert pools, integrates fusion methods with and without additional training, and offers a unified evaluation suite. The pools contain domain experts independently trained from Qwen3 and Llama base models across math, code, science, instruction following, and tool use. The suite evaluates capability retention and computational cost on eight public benchmarks under comparable data access rules, reporting normalized average and worst performance, runtime, and peak GPU memory usage. Extensive experiments show that training-free methods preserve reasoning performance but substantially degrade tool use at both model scales. In contrast, MOPD preserves tool use and achieves a more balanced capability profile, but requires additional data preparation, inference, and training. Jointly retaining reasoning, instruction following, and tool use at low computational cost remains a challenge. We hope FusionEval supports future research on efficient model fusion that jointly preserves expert capabilities. Our code is available at https://github.com/Baicaihaochi/FusionEval_Benchmark.

MemPlan: Memory-Conditioned PDDL Planning for Partially Observable Text Environments
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Tengwei Song, Robert Hoehndorf

发布日期：Sat,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2610.30379v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LLM agents are flexible in language interaction, but using them as long-horizon planners is costly and prone to hallucinated or invalid actions. This paper targets a different role for LLMs: constrained bridges between language and symbolic planning. We propose MemPlan, a memory-conditioned PDDL planning framework for partially observable interactive text environments. MemPlan keeps the PDDL domain fixed and reconstructs the PDDL problem at each step from parsed observations, candidate hypotheses, and interaction memory. Positive graph memory assigns relevance-based action costs to candidate hypotheses for missing objects, locations, or conditions, guiding the planner toward more plausible checks. Negative memory records execution-refuted assumptions and compiles them into exclusion constraints, preventing repeated attempts at candidates already contradicted by feedback. A unified schema-conditioned language--symbol interface, trained offline with supervised fine-tuning, connects text environments to the planning loop through observation-to-fact parsing and symbolic-action-to-command grounding. Experiments on TextWorld, ALFWorld, and Robotouille show that MemPlan improves task completion and step efficiency over language-only and PDDL-aware baselines, while substantially reducing online token cost compared with LLM-planning baselines such as ReAct. The same fine-tuned interface is also reused across TextWorld task types without task-specific fine-tuning. Our code and data are available at: https://github.com/tewiSong/MemPlan.

研究问题：LLM代理在长时规划中成本高且易产生幻觉或无效动作。 主要方法：提出MemPlan框架，结合固定PDDL领域和动态问题重构，使用正负图记忆引导规划。 关键贡献：在部分可观测文本环境中提升任务完成率和步骤效率，大幅降低在线token成本。

Behind Aggregate Scores: What Systematically Varying Arena-Hard's LLM Judge Reveals
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.46)

作者：Louise Yorke

发布日期：Sat,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2610.30512v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LLM judges are used to determine which of two answers is better. Trusting these verdicts requires knowing how repeatable they are and how much they depend on the judge's instructions. We show that similar aggregate scores can conceal substantial changes in individual verdicts, both when the same setup is repeated and when the instructions change. Using 500 Arena-Hard v2.0 prompts with two models' answers held fixed, we run GPT-4.1 as the judge under all eight combinations of criteria, output format and task framing, and repeat every setup unchanged at temperature zero on a pinned model version. Rerunning the default setup moves the aggregate score by about one point but changes the win/tie/loss outcome on 21.2% of prompts. Instruction changes introduce further sensitivity: in one setup, which asks for the verdict without reasoning and also drops several other instructions, outcomes change on 28.2 percentage points more prompts than the two setups' unchanged reruns do on average, and the aggregate score falls by 4.09 points. In another, where the criteria prioritise accuracy, outcomes change on 6.7 percentage points more prompts by the same comparison, while the aggregate score barely moves. A stable aggregate score therefore does not establish that individual verdicts are repeatable. This distinction matters when those verdicts are used as preference labels or to assess individual responses. Evaluations that rely on individual verdicts should repeat each setup they use and report both aggregate scores and prompt-level agreement.

Compiling Autoregressive LLM Control into Policy-Exact Parallel Inference for Hierarchical RAG
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.43)

作者：Zehao Lin, Qianfeng Shen, Xixuan Hao, Shaobo Cui, Chunyu Li, Kai Chen, Bo Tang, Zhenjun Wang, Jin Yan, Zhiyu Li, Feiyu Xiong

发布日期：Sat,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2610.30664v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models increasingly act as controllers in structured decision pipelines, yet their decisions are still executed by autoregressive text decoding. Hierarchical RAG is one such setting: at each frontier of a document tree, an LLM commits to $b$ of $k$ sections under an input-dependent action grammar, executed as either failure-prone free-form JSON or reliable but strictly serial grammar-constrained generation. We show that this serialization is unnecessary for bounded-horizon control: the reachable future forms a shallow action-prefix tree, which we compile --- one root prefill plus one tree-causal probe evaluate every prefix-conditioned action distribution with serial decoding's tokens, positions, and causal ancestry. A prefix-state lemma makes compiled control policy-exact in exact arithmetic: the same policy with a two-forward critical path and zero output tokens. One further probe pass certifies the exact order-marginalized MAP set. Across four document-disjoint splits and two scales on the primary MetaX device, compiled control matches the serial policy on $84$--$89\%$ of questions --- every instrumented divergence a floating-point near-tie --- and cuts control latency by $35$--$47\%$ versus token-wise serial decoding and $6$--$7\%$ versus an idealized chunked engine; an H800 repetition preserves the ordering.
