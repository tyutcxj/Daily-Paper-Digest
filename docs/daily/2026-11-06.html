EnvOpt: Automated Optimization for Agent Environments
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.60)

作者：Mingxuan Li, Huanzhi Mao, Qiuyang Mang, Hanchen Li, Joseph E. Gonzalez, Alex Dimakis

发布日期：Fri,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.07372v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: An LLM agent's cost and success depend on its model, its harness, and its environment. In addition to the widely studied directions of parameter tuning and harness optimization, we propose the third direction: optimization over stationary environments. Agents are often deployed in rich environments shared by many tasks, such as a company's Slack workspace or a complex collection of data sources, documents or tickets. We call these environments stationary environments. We introduce EnvOpt, a method for automated end-to-end optimization of stationary environments for any given model and harness, using real or synthetic tasks without needing any graders or reference answers. On AutomationBench, EnvOpt reduces the cost of Claude Haiku 4.5 by 72.7% and raises its pass rate by 14.34%. The same environment transfers to Opus 5, cutting its cost by 68.1% with a 1.34% gain. Added on top of Meta-Harness's optimized harness for Haiku 4.5, it lowers cost by a further 83.4% and adds 11.17% accuracy over Meta-Harness alone. Across 5 benchmarks, EnvOpt significantly reduces the cost of agents while improving or maintaining pass rate, and is transferable to different models and different harnesses. We also discuss common successful and rejected environment optimization strategies during automated optimization. More broadly, our results show that how information is represented to an agent shapes both its cost and its success, making the environment worth optimizing in addition to the parameters and the harness.

研究问题：如何通过优化静态环境降低LLM agent成本并提高成功率？ 主要方法：EnvOpt自动端到端优化环境，无需评分器或参考答案。 关键贡献：显著降低成本（如Haiku 4.5降72.7%），提升通过率，环境可迁移至不同模型和harness。

Octopus: One Brain, Many Branches for Latent-KV Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.55)

作者：Tianming Sha

发布日期：Fri,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2611.06956v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multi-agent LLM systems explore in parallel by spawning sub-agents with isolated context windows, whereas single-agent systems append every search and verification step into one growing trajectory. However, existing multi-agent frameworks duplicate prefix KV caches across branches, exchange findings by re-prefilling text summaries, and flatten or drop cross-branch dependencies during reinforcement learning (RL). We introduce Octopus, a single-model multi-agent RL framework in which all threads share one backbone and coordinate over a GPU-resident directed acyclic graph (DAG) of KV states through five primitives (fork, read_thread, wait_threads, rewind, commit). Octopus combines three co-designed mechanisms: (1) Zero-Copy Radix Forking, which spawns branches as $\mathcal{O}(1)$ cursor views over shared physical KV pages; (2) Attention-Only KV Mounting, which mounts a sibling thread's terminal KV blocks directly into full-attention layers while bypassing linear-recurrent states (Gated DeltaNet / Mamba-2), cutting off-task hijacking by $21\times$ ($57.9\% \to 2.7\%$) while retaining $92.2\%$ of full-text mutual information at zero extra KV allocation; and (3) a Packed DAG-GRPO Trainer, which packs all episode branches into one block-sparse attention pass and assigns counterfactual branch credit via leave-one-branch-out marginal rewards. Trained end-to-end with GRPO on Qwen3.5-4B over a sparse BM25 corpus, Octopus reaches $49.6\%$ accuracy on BrowseComp-Plus ($+17.5$ points over single-agent GRPO at 143K context and $+12.5$ points over text-summary multi-agent RL) and $51.2\%$ on OpenResearcher, outperforming dense-retrieval agents up to $250\times$ larger while increasing training throughput $3.3\times$ and cutting peak KV memory $2.8\times$.

研究问题：多智能体LLM系统中KV缓存重复、跨分支依赖丢失等问题。 主要方法：提出Octopus框架，通过零拷贝Radix分叉、仅注意力KV挂载和打包DAG-GRPO训练器实现高效多智能体RL。 关键贡献：实现O(1)分支创建、减少任务劫持21倍、保留92.2%全文本互信息且零额外KV分配。

摘要

arXiv:2611.07392v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present \textbf{WeLM-Image}, a unified image foundation model that integrates multimodal understanding into text-to-image generation, instruction-guided editing, and multi-reference synthesis. A Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) backbone combines shared attention with independent understanding and generation parameters to limit optimization interference. We optimize generation and editing separately with rubric- and checklist-based rewards, then consolidate the specialized policies through noise-aware on-policy distillation. In the reported overall comparisons, the 1536-resolution model ranks first among the evaluated open-source models on PRISM-Bench, Qwen-Image-Bench, and UnicBench. PromptOps: Iterative Prompt Optimization for LLM-as-a-Judge Systems
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.50)

作者：Sven Kleinkop, Tam\'as Janusko, Ricardo Bochnia, Gunnar Hempel, Steffen Tomschke, J\"urgen Anke, Maik Thiele

发布日期：Fri,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.07123v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly used as automated judges for evaluating the behavior of chatbots and other AI systems. In deployment, however, their agreement with the intended human evaluation policy may degrade as user interactions and score distributions change. Existing automatic prompt optimization methods are primarily studied as one-shot procedures on static development sets and therefore provide limited guidance for maintaining LLM judges over time. We frame this problem as judge drift and introduce PromptOps, a closedloop framework for monitoring and iteratively recalibrating LLM-as-a-Judge (LLMaaJ) systems. PromptOps separates four operational decisions: when to optimize, which optimization procedure to apply, which feedback records to use, and how to update the judge prompt. We instantiate the framework with five prompt optimization methods: GEPA, SIMBA, Rubric Synthesis, and exemplar selection using either simulated annealing or a Tree-structured Parzen Estimator. Our experiments demonstrate that iterative prompt optimization can recalibrate LLM judges and recover performance after controlled shifts in the referencescore distribution. However, effective maintenance is inherently contextdependent: neither optimization algorithm choice nor update timing generalizes universally across settings. These findings establish prompt optimization for deployed LLM judges as an inherently iterative maintenance problem and demonstrate that PromptOps provides a practical abstraction for studying and implementing this process.

相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.07408v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Panoptic Scene Graph generation (PSG) requires holistic yet fine-grained reasoning over complex relations among objects. However, current Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) applied to PSG suffer from a perception bottleneck: they function as static observers limited by fixed-resolution views. As a result, models often overlook critical relations involving small or peripheral objects and struggle to differentiate ambiguous predicates in complex scenes. To address these limitations, we propose PSGAgent, an agentic framework that enhances visual perception for PSG through a think-act-observe loop. PSGAgent allows MLLMs to dynamically refine their visual perception using two tools: a Region Zoom-In Tool for local exploration of small or boundary relations and a Visual Highlight Tool for relation-focused segmentation that filters out irrelevant contextual noise. To optimize decision-making, we construct a two-stage training pipeline. First, we create a high-quality, error-aware Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (M-CoT) dataset that provides robust supervision for cold-start learning. Then, we design a novel hierarchical reward mechanism integrated with Group Sequence Policy Optimization (GSPO) to enable strategic and adaptive tool usage. Extensive experiments on the PSG benchmark show that PSGAgent achieves SOTA performance, demonstrating strong capability in both holistic and fine-grained scene understanding.
