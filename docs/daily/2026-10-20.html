Persistent Memory and Iterative Improvement in an LLM Agent for Chinese-Character Drawing
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.60)

作者：Peilin Wu, Yi-Zhuang You

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.24090v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Does persistent memory accumulated from previous attempts help an LLM agent solve new problems, and does memory design affect that benefit? We study programmatic Chinese-character drawing across five agent conditions attempting the same 668 items. Accumulated memory produces modest aggregate gains despite substantial growth in the memory stores: free-form notes improve first-attempt success by 4.6 percentage points, while a design combining free-form memory with reusable code improves it by 2.8 points. The two designs do not differ significantly overall, but the code bank shows a suggestive advantage on later, harder items. A separate post-hoc comparison finds a much larger improvement when the code-bank agent receives privileged, target-specific structural supervision. The agent can therefore exploit highly informative structural guidance, although the privileged per-item information does not establish what could have been learned from experience alone. These results suggest that memory design may affect where accumulated experience becomes useful, even when its average benefit is modest.

I$^2$-Med: Interpretable Medical Inference through Visual-Guided Dynamic Logits Calibration
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.55)

作者：Md Sajid, Shreeyut Maheshwari, Akshat Mishra, Ritik Mishra, Mohammad Tanveer

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2610.23858v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Medical vision language models have demonstrated strong potential for medical image understanding and reasoning. However, their interpretability and reliability remain limited because medical vision language tasks require precise visual grounding and clinically coherent explanations. This is particularly challenging due to complex imaging patterns and scarce expert annotations. These challenges reduce the effectiveness of conventional reasoning methods and lead to visual hallucination, defined as the generation of text that is not grounded in the given visual input, which further limits their applicability in clinical practice. To mitigate these challenges, we propose an interpretable medical inference (I$^2$-Med) framework that enhances visual faithfulness during inference without additional training to improve reasoning and explanation faithfulness in medical vision language models. During text generation, I$^2$-Med uses a visual guidance module to check whether each candidate word is supported by the medical image and consistent with the current reasoning. Based on this guidance, the model's output scores are adjusted to favor visually grounded words, without changing the model parameters. Extensive experiments on the OmniMedVQA benchmark, covering eight medical imaging modalities and five clinical question types, demonstrate that our proposed I$^2$-Med framework generates more clinically meaningful and visually faithful reasoning chains and reduces visual hallucination under standard medical VQA evaluation metrics. The source code is publicly available at \url{https://github.com/mtanveer1/l2-med}.

研究问题：医学视觉语言模型可解释性差，存在视觉幻觉。 主要方法：I$^2-Med框架，推理时用视觉引导模块动态校准logits，偏向视觉基础词。 关键贡献：无需额外训练，增强视觉忠实度，提升推理和解释的可靠性。

miniTE: Diagnosing and Bridging the Execution Gap in Native NVFP4 Reinforcement Learning
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.53)

作者：Zhen Li, Mingfa Feng, Yiming Zhang, Yang Yu, Changhai Zhou, Pengxiang Li, Dongkuan Wu, Congkai Xie, Hongxia Yang

发布日期：Tue,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2610.23928v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Native NVFP4 promises to accelerate reinforcement learning (RL) for large language models (LLMs), but achieving stable learning across separate training and sampling engines remains challenging. We show that matching model weights and numerical formats is insufficient to ensure consistent policy evaluation, highlighting the importance of cross-engine numerical execution. We introduce miniTE, a lightweight framework that combines execution diagnostics, cross-engine numerical alignment, and efficient native W4A4 (4-bit weights and activations) GEMM computation for LLM RL. It improves numerical execution consistency without modifying the RL objective, while reducing implementation overhead to realize the benefits of native low-bit computation. Long-horizon RL experiments on Qwen3-30B-A3B demonstrate stable training and benchmark performance close to BF16, outperforming naive Transformer Engine (TE) NVFP4 in evaluation quality. On single NVIDIA B300 GPU node, the optimized learner reaches 33,540 tokens/s, yielding 1.39x and 1.47x the throughput of BF16 and TE NVFP4, respectively, for complete learner updates. We released the framework, kernels, diagnostics, and training configurations to make native low-bit RL easier.

arXiv:2610.23926v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fake accounts continue to undermine online platforms, yet it remains unclear whether multimodal learning offers meaningful improvements over strong metadata baselines or graph-based approaches. In this work, we study bot detection by integrating textual, visual, and account metadata signals on the TwiBot-22 benchmark using a subsample of 30,000 users. We propose a multimodal framework combining pretrained textual (XLM-RoBERTa) and visual (CLIP) embeddings with structured account-level features such as follower counts, posting frequency, and account age, and evaluate multiple modality combinations using CatBoost alongside a Relational Graph Convolutional Network (R-GCN). Overall, our findings suggest that under practical constraints, feature quality and metadata richness matter more than fusion complexity.

摘要

arXiv:2610.23946v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Pretraining scaling laws treat training data as a one-dimensional quantity: a token count. arXiv:2610.23939v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The size of the KV cache grows linearly with context length, making it a major memory bottleneck in LLM serving. Motivated by recent progress in identifying the functional roles of attention heads, a growing body of work compresses the KV cache non-uniformly across heads. Existing methods either fix the important heads via offline profiling or select them using attention signals from the prefill stage. In both cases, however, accuracy fluctuates substantially across workloads. While investigating the cause of this instability, we find that the active heads shift between the prefill and decode stages even within a single input. Along the decode path, by contrast, the activehead composition remains stable across content tokens once format tokens are filtered out. Based on this observation, we propose PeekKV. PeekKV observes a single content token immediately after prefill - a one-token peek - to jointly obtain head-group scores and token-importance scores. Guided by these scores, PeekKV allocates KV cache budgets to head groups in proportion to their group scores and retains only the top-scoring tokens within each budget. PeekKV requires no offline profiling or training and thus applies directly to unseen workloads and new models. Across four public benchmarks, PeekKV achieves the highest accuracy in more than half of the per-model comparisons against state-of-the-art methods, and stays closest to the full-cache accuracy on RULER and LongBench. On Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, PeekKV retains 95.7% of the full-cache accuracy at a 10% memory budget on RULER with 16K contexts and 95.9% at a 1.5% budget on LongBench.

arXiv:2610.23864v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Adaptive VLM ensembles save computation by stopping early on easy images and escalating harder ones. Although a single conformal threshold can make the final predictor marginally valid, it can still fail severely along individual computational routes. We characterize this failure precisely: if a route has prevalence pi, marginal 1-alpha coverage allows its conditional coverage to fall to max{0, 1-alpha/pi}. To address this mismatch, we learn the router on development data, freeze all routing choices, and calibrate the returned score separately for early exits and escalated inputs. We call this pipeline SAGE-CP. Its calibration step applies Mondrian split conformal prediction to the operational groups created by the router. Under exchangeability, the resulting prediction sets achieve finite-sample coverage within each route. Across 10 benchmarks, 4 frozen VLMs, and 5 aligned splits, both global calibration and SAGE-CP achieve 0.900 marginal coverage at a 0.90 target, but their route behavior differs substantially. SAGE-CP raises mean worst-route coverage from 0.623 to 0.887 and reduces the mean route-coverage gap from 0.324 to 0.025, using the same routed scores and 1.177 source calls per image. The stronger guarantee increases the mean prediction-set size from 1.893 to 2.212; nevertheless, the sets remain smaller than those of the full ensemble (2.486) while requiring 70.6 percent fewer source calls. The route-level reliability persists across 6 fixed query fractions, while finer exit-depth groups demonstrate the need for additional calibration data on rare routes. Across 5 datasets, SAGE-CP also reduces measured batch-one latency by 58.8 to 63.4 percent and gross GPU energy by 55.7 to 57.5 percent relative to full inference. Thus, routing provides computational savings, while route-stratified calibration attaches coverage guarantees to the operational paths created by the router.
