Behavior Beyond Text: Evaluating Refusal Transfer Failure in LLM Agents
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.60)

作者：Ekagra Gupta, Raj Saha, Aniekan-Abasi Inyang, Adam Al Gharib

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.28982v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: While large language models are increasingly deployed as tool-calling agents, their safety evaluations still focus primarily on their ability to refuse harmful requests in text. We investigate whether the refusal behavior in text transfers to tool use. We construct a benchmark of 2,304 prompts across four domains, consisting of matched triplets that present the same harmful or benign request in text-only, standard tool-enabled, and adversarially framed tool-enabled settings. We then assess tool-call safety using 20 predefined forbidden actions. Testing five models of varying sizes and families, we find that requests refused in text frequently result in unsafe tool calls. Under standard tool-enabled prompting, this divergence occurs for 10-52 percent of harmful requests refused in chat. To mechanistically investigate this gap, we identify a refusal-associated direction in the residual stream of each of the five models. We find that in tool-enabled contexts, the activations along this direction decrease in four of five models. Furthermore, lower activation predicts unsafe tool calls across all five models (AUC 0.76-0.86). However, restoring the signal to the respective text-only value through activation patching only prevents a subset of unsafe calls, primarily by suppressing tool use rather than by producing safer calls. Steering along the refusal direction nearly eliminates unsafe calls only at strengths that also suppress benign tool use or degrade the output quality. Our findings indicate that the identified refusal direction contributes to whether a model acts, but only partially explains the refusal divergence. Therefore, evaluating the safety of tool-calling agents requires directly examining their actions along with their textual responses.

AI 总结

研究问题：LLM代理在文本拒绝有害请求的能力是否转移到工具使用中 主要方法：构建2304个提示的基准，包含文本、标准工具、对抗工具三种设置，测试五个模型 关键贡献：发现文本拒绝的请求在工具使用中常导致不安全调用，识别了拒绝相关方向在工具上下文中的激活下降

SimHack: Predicting the onset of detectable reward hacking before RL training
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.57)

作者：Junwei Deng, Alex Bie, Natalia Ponomareva

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2610.28707v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: When training LLMs to code with reinforcement learning, reward hacking is often detectable but difficult to prevent, since (a) environment hardening is increasingly difficult as training environments grow more complex; and (b) training-time interventions incentivize models to obfuscate hacking behavior to bypass detection. We instead study whether we can predict when detectable reward hacking becomes dominant before executing an RL run, enabling us to select lower-risk recipes in advance. We find that the initial hacking rate alone does not determine onset, which varies substantially across training recipes and repeated runs. We thus formulate this problem as predicting the distribution of the reward hacking onset step under a target training recipe. We introduce SimHack, a simulator that learns reward hacking training dynamics from a handful of smaller-scale reference RL runs and generates counterfactual simulation runs of unseen target recipes without costly retraining. Across unseen target recipes, SimHack predicts the median onset to a relative error of 11.3% and achieves across-recipe rank correlation of 0.85, while generalizing to differences in model scale, curriculum, number of rollouts, and learning rate between reference and target recipes. As an application, we show that SimHack can guide training decisions by screening candidate RL curricula, discovering curricula over the same set of scenarios that train for 96.6% longer without hacking.

PerMIT: Securing Persistent Memory for LLM Agents via Machine-Checked Integrity Theorems and Scalable Community Trust
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.53)

作者：Tanqiu Jiang, Min Bai, Sadat Shahriar, Wanyu Du, Sandesh Swamy

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.28987v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Persistent memory is a largely undefended surface for injection and poisoning, and the same risk limits memory sharing among agents. We present PerMIT, a memory architecture with provable integrity guarantees under an explicit trust assumption: only user-typed inputs and values confirmed at a gate are trusted. PerMIT mediates the release and use of untrusted environment-derived values through a finite memory interface, while preserving agent-driven multi-turn planning and tool choice. Over this finite interface, we prove two integrity theorems that are also machine-checked in Lean 4, which together ensure that environment-derived memory reaches a sensitive action only if confirmed at the gate and influences the agent's behavior only through what the gate displays. Every attack in our threat model thereby reduces to a single auditable decision, which a human, an LLM verifier, or both can make in practice. On Trojan Hippo, PerMIT records zero observed attack success with self-verification and lightweight verifiers. Because each confirmation is a logged verdict on exact content, endorsements accumulate across users, and sufficiently endorsed items become community-confirmed with no further verification required. Under explicit assumptions, we prove and corroborate in simulation a co-scaling property: as the community grows, the risks of promoting poison and of wrongly evicting benign knowledge fall, while per-user verification effort does not rise. On $\tau^2$-bench, communities of up to 90 autonomous agents use PerMIT to build shared knowledge bases with no direct memory injection promoted, and the resulting knowledge significantly improves new agents on held-out tasks, suggesting a path toward agents that safely self-improve at test time.

AI 总结

研究问题：持久内存面临注入和投毒攻击，限制代理间共享。 主要方法：提出PerMIT架构，通过有限内存接口和机器验证的完整性定理，结合门控确认和社区信任。 关键贡献：证明两个完整性定理，实现零攻击成功率，并通过社区确认减少验证开销。

To control degradation as sparsity increases, we introduce LADDER (Layer-Wise Allocation via Damage-Driven Evaluation and Refinement), which allocates sparsity incrementally. At each step, it evaluates pruning an additional fraction of weights from each eligible layer and commits the layer update that causes the least end-to-end degradation per removed parameter. This process produces non-uniform sparsity profiles and exactly nested masks across multiple budgets. We evaluate LADDER on five OPT, Llama, and Qwen models across 10-90% sparsity, using SparseGPT as its candidate-generation backend and comparing against uniform and adaptive allocation baselines. Results reveal a clear operating-regime trade-off: LADDER is comparable or slightly weaker at benign sparsity but becomes substantially more robust from 70% onward. This high-sparsity advantage largely persists after posterior distillation across four of five models and translates into improved retention of aggregate zero-shot accuracy.
