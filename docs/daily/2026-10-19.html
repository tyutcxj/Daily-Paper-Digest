SAG2: Embedding-Free Multi-Hop Retrieval with BM25 and JEV
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.57)

作者：Yuchao Wu

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2610.22588v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Strong multi-hop retrieval should be practical to deploy without expensive corpus preparation or complex database infrastructure. We present SAG2, an embedding-free retrieval architecture built around a single SQLite database, BM25, and contextual evidence decisions from JEV. SAG2 follows the query-time association principle of SAG (SQL-Retrieval Augmented Generation), while replacing its event-entity and dense indexes with a lexical index over original passages. Selected passages supply terms for further retrieval, and newly retrieved evidence guides subsequent decisions. This moves semantic evidence selection to query time, eliminating offline embedding computation, LLM-based event-entity extraction, and dedicated database servers. The resulting system combines lightweight preparation with strong retrieval accuracy: on 1,000-question subsets of MuSiQue, 2WikiMultiHopQA, and HotpotQA, SAG2 achieves the highest Recall@5 and Recall@10 among the compared methods, with Recall@10 of 86.62%, 97.43%, and 99.00%. It also reaches 81.10% document Recall@10 over the 511,962-document EnterpriseRAG-Bench corpus. Indexing all 11,656 MuSiQue passages takes 2.36 seconds, produces a 25.87 MiB database, and requires no model calls. SAG2 demonstrates that accurate multi-hop evidence retrieval can be supported by compact, rapidly prepared infrastructure, making it a practical foundation for knowledge retrieval applications.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何实现轻量、高效的多跳证据检索，避免嵌入和复杂基础设施。 主要方法：提出SAG2架构，基于SQLite、BM25和JEV进行查询时语义证据选择。 关键贡献：无嵌入多跳检索，在MuSiQue等数据集上Recall@10达86.62%-99.00%，索引快速且数据库紧凑。

Lean4AgenticRL: Advancing Agentic RL with Verifiable Multi-level Process Rewards
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.52)

作者：Ruida Wang, Gerald Friedland, Tong Zhang, Chris Kong

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2610.22448v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Enhancing the agentic capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) to tackle complex software engineering (SWE) and general terminal-usage problems is a pressing challenge. Current state-of-the-art LLMs often go through large-scale agentic Reinforcement Learning (RL). However, they mostly rely on high-level outcome rewards, with minimal consideration of low-level toolcall and mid-level workflow-stage rewards, which makes the training less effective. To overcome this limitation, we propose **Lean4AgenticRL**, which, to the best of our knowledge, is the first framework that grounds multi-level process rewards in the unified Lean4 formalization schema for open-ended agentic RL. **Lean4AgenticRL** first builds a *problem-specific workflow* system that balances the agent's own capability and controllability during execution, laying the foundation for further verification. Subsequently, our workflow formalization and multi-level reward annotation methods enable agent behavior verification within a general harness system. It provides multi-level rewards to support more effective agentic RL with the workflow-stage-normalized GRPO algorithm. Using **Lean4AgenticRL**, we train **LAR-9B/27B**, a family of models with enhanced agentic capability. Extensive experiments indicate that our model outperforms the leading model of similar size. **LAR-27B** achieves **77.3%** on TerminalBench-2.1. On average, our models outperform the base model by **9.89%** and surpass the second-best model of comparable size we know by **5.07%**. These results indicate the potential of integrating formal methods in enhancing open-ended agentic RL.

AI 总结

研究问题：增强LLM在软件工程和终端使用中的代理能力，现有方法缺乏多级过程奖励。 主要方法：提出Lean4AgenticRL框架，基于Lean4形式化方案构建工作流系统，实现多级过程奖励和归一化GRPO算法。 关键贡献：首次统一多级过程奖励于Lean4形式化框架，训练LAR-9B/27B模型，显著提升代理能力。

AuditKIT: A Unified Evaluation Spine for Benchmarking, Judging, and Comparing Large Language Models and Agentic Systems
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.50)

作者：Shreeyans Arora, Utsav Avaiya, Zera Lyngkhoi, Hem Gosalia, Ram Mohan Rao Kadiyala, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu, Pratinav Seth

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.CL

摘要

arXiv:2610.22511v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Evaluating language models and agents requires combining benchmark accuracy, judge-based assessment, runtime performance, tool-use behavior, and retrieval quality. Differences in interfaces, metric semantics, and trace formats complicate these comparisons and can hide failures in agentic workflows. We present AuditKIT, a source-available evaluation library that records these measurements in one result representation, with direction-aware metric contracts, configuration fingerprints, and extensible metrics and backends. For agents, it provides turn-aware tool-call matching, separates parallel from sequential execution, and distinguishes verified task outcomes from trajectory-level diagnostics. Missing or unobservable evidence is marked unscored rather than counted as failure. For retrieval-augmented generation, it combines ranked retrieval metrics, judged grounding, and deterministic evidence checks. Seven case studies spanning model comparison, serving runtimes, retrieval, tool calling, and interoperability show how answer extraction, runtime behavior, and metric selection change evaluation outcomes.

AI 总结

研究问题：评估语言模型和智能体系统时，指标差异和格式不统一导致比较困难且易隐藏工作流失败。 主要方法：提出AuditKIT开源评估库，统一记录测量结果，包含方向感知指标契约、配置指纹、可扩展后端，支持智能体工具调用匹配和RAG评估。 关键贡献：提供统一的评估框架，区分任务结果与轨迹诊断，标记缺失证据而非计为失败，通过七项案例验证其有效性。

arXiv:2610.22543v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Diffusion language models (DLMs) promise parallel text generation, but in practice face an unresolved speed-quality trade-off. Exact decoding fills in only one masked position per model pass, while faster multi-token decoding schemes are lossy. Speculative decoding is the standard lossless acceleration for autoregressive models. However, it does not transfer directly to DLMs, which must choose both what to generate and where. We show that this order is the missing piece: the model's own predictions from the previous pass already give the right token at 92-100% of positions, but not which position comes next. Based on this observation, we introduce Speculating Positions Order Trajectory (SPOT), which learns to predict this order. A 0.62M-parameter model reads only the target's output distributions and proposes a tree of candidate continuations, and the target verifies all of them in a single pass by checking whether each equals its own next state. arXiv:2610.22594v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Medical multi-agent systems, in which LLM agents discuss a case, are increasingly studied for clinical decision support. Prompt injection, text that an attacker adds to a model's input, can manipulate LLMs into wrong decisions. How it affects these systems, and how to protect them, remains unclear. Across two systems, two model families and three benchmarks, a single injected passage succeeds in up to 61% of cases, and collaboration does not consistently protect against it. Existing defenses help only partly: they miss injections that look like ordinary clinical text. Yet when asked to check the injected text against the patient's findings, a model that followed the injection usually rejects it. We therefore propose a training-free defense that verifies the clinical judgments behind the system's decision. Following clinical practice, it separates the patient's findings from clinical judgments, such as diagnoses, recommendations and instructions, and checks each judgment against the findings alone, so the decision rests only on supported judgments. Our defense outperforms all baselines on every benchmark, lowering attack success on MedQA to 4-7% while maintaining accuracy on cases without an attack, and remains effective with a medical model and against defense-aware and adaptive attacks.

摘要

arXiv:2610.22618v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models rely on spurious features in clinical classification: swapping a single gender token in clinical notes alters gender-irrelevant diagnoses in up to 53.5% of cases. Existing inference-time corrections either need counterfactual data built around the specific spurious feature or behave unpredictably. We observe that how a model's preference between answers evolves across layers, its prediction trajectory, indicates for each document whether a correction is likely to help. We propose Trajectory-Guided Steering (TGS), which decides from the trajectory alone whether to correct each prediction and in which direction, with no counterfactual data and no knowledge of the spurious feature. On 16 clinical classification tasks from i2b2, TGS fixes 17.7% of errors while breaking only 1.3% of correct predictions and stays net-positive across all tested hyperparameter settings; all baselines are net-negative or near zero. The same corrections reduce reliance on the spurious feature: on the most affected diseases, gender-swap inconsistency falls sharply (hypertension 53.5% -> 38.0%, diabetes 35.1% -> 21.5%). Applying the correction to every document instead raises breaks on correct positive predictions from 0.1% to 6.4%, so per-document selection is essential. TGS also transfers to three model families and three other clinical and biomedical datasets.
