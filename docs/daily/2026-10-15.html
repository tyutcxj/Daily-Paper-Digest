Lotus: Learning to Understand Time-series by Learning to Forecast
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.59)

作者：Van Dai Do, Huu Hiep Nguyen, Minh Hoang Nguyen, Manh Nguyen, Hung Le

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2610.18877v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Time-series language models typically align numerical signals with language using labels, captions, or question-answer pairs. Although such supervision encourages linguistic representations, it does not explicitly require them to preserve information about how the underlying time-series signal evolves. We introduce forecast grounding, a pretraining principle that requires language-aligned time-series representations to remain predictive of the future evolution before they are adapted to downstream tasks. We instantiate this principle in LOTUS, which divides each time-series channel into non-overlapping patches and maps them into the LLM embedding space. It then predicts the next patch from the LLM hidden states of the time-series tokens, encouraging the learned representation to retain temporal information. To capture both temporal changes and periodic patterns, we also propose a temporal-spectral patch representation that combines each raw time-series patch with its spectral representation before mapping it into the LLM embedding space. Across three language model backbones and six downstream evaluation datasets, LOTUS achieves the best average performance with minimal trainable parameters, reaching 59.91% accuracy and outperforming the strongest matched baseline by 3.36%. Our representation analyses further show that LOTUS uses the numerical input and preserves temporal structure in its learned patch representations. These results establish forecast grounding as a promising pretraining principle, enabling compact time-series language models to outperform models 25 to 50 times their size on TSRBench.

研究问题：时间序列语言模型未明确要求保留信号演化信息。 主要方法：提出预测接地原则，LOTUS通过非重叠补丁映射并预测下一补丁，结合时频谱表示。 关键贡献：LOTUS在多个骨干和数据集上达最佳平均性能（59.91%），超越基线3.36%，参数最少。

arXiv:2610.19108v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have been widely applied in embodied agents and robotics for vision tasks. However, MLLMs suffer from limitations in spatial reasoning and perception. Post-training techniques have shown promise for improving these capabilities; yet, there are limited data-driven surveys of such approaches. In this survey, we consider approaches to fine-tuning pretrained MLLMs for spatial reasoning tasks, ranging from supervised to reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on the datasets used. We investigate themes of dataset construction, data modality and annotation, and evaluation of the resulting models. We find four main methods for constructing fixed spatial annotations: rules and templates, LLM prompting, procedural generation, and human-in-the-loop. We also observe substantial variation in evaluation approaches, including different scoring methods, tolerance metrics, and benchmark types. Finally, we identify opportunities for future research in unified dynamic annotation, efficient spatial reasoning, standardization of evaluation, expanded spatial range, and human-centered spatial interactions.

摘要

arXiv:2610.19052v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Video-based remote physiological measurement (RPM) has broad applications, while Vision-Language Models (VLMs) provide a promising opportunity to enable end-to-end physiological monitoring for future human-aware intelligent systems. However, existing RPM methods rely on task-specific models and cannot be seamlessly integrated with the multimodal understanding and interaction capabilities of VLMs. We revisit RPM with VLMs and provide empirical evidence for two major bottlenecks: generic visual semantic representations fail to preserve fine-grained physiological cues; while discrete token prediction in autoregressive generation is inherently misaligned with continuous numerical measurement. To address these challenges, we propose VITAL (Visual-physiological Token Alignment for Language Models), a visual-physiological token alignment framework for RPM and preserving general vision tasks. VITAL retains the original visual pathway for semantic understanding and introduces an independent physiological pathway to jointly model semantic and physiological information. Moreover, we propose a structured numerical alignment approach to improve the reliability of generative heart rate estimation by enhancing numerical relationship modeling. Extensive experiments show that VITAL improves cross-domain estimation performance on multiple rPPG datasets while preserving the general visual understanding capability of VLMs, demonstrating its effectiveness as a unified visual-physiological model. Code will be released.

Cross-Modal Latent Influence: Patch-Level Training Sample Attribution for Vision-Language Models
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.48)

作者：Laura Yao, Shixing Yu, Jie Peng, Promit Ghosal, Kyra Gan, Tianlong Chen

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.14190v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Explaining a vision--language model (VLM) prediction requires tracing training-example influence to the multimodal evidence that mediates it, yet existing attribution methods largely operate only at the sample level. Recent latent-mediated influence methods provide feature-level attribution in language models, but lack the multimodal and spatial structure needed to ground influence in VLMs. We introduce MOSAIC (Multimodal Orthogonalized Sparse Autoencoders for Influence Characterization). It learns a shared sparse image--text latent space whose concepts are 1) sparse within each modality, 2) supported on contiguous image regions, 3) aligned to the phrases that describe them, and 4) approximately decorrelated. It then applies EK-FAC to decompose training-example influence through these concepts and localize their contributions to specific text phrases and image regions. We evaluate MOSAIC on Qwen and Gemma across captioning, chest X-ray understanding, and visual question answering. The learned features are more localized and coherent without degrading task behavior, and ablating influence-ranked latents changes model outputs more than ablating latents ranked by activation or frequency. We find that visual evidence can substantially reorganize training-data attribution while preserving more of the shared latent structure. 相关度 ★★★☆☆ (0.39)

作者：Xiaofan Li, Fengchun Qiao, Xing Gao

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.14334v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In Model Context Protocol (MCP) systems, LLMs select tools from metadata rather than from their implementations. Names and descriptions summarize what a tool does, while the parameter schema specifies the fields a tool accepts and how they are organized. Our analysis of metadata for 367,174 MCP tools reveals substantial schema variation among functionally similar tools. Many of these tools share names and similar descriptions but expose different parameter schemas. This raises a key question: can parameter-schema differences influence tool selection before any call is made? We study this question through controlled metadata interventions across five LLMs and five tasks. Each comparison changes exactly one metadata factor (server name, tool name, description, or parameter schema) while keeping the others fixed. We balance tool positions and use conditional logistic regression to estimate position-adjusted effects. We find that: (1) parameter-schema changes produce the largest and most consistent selection shifts among the tested interventions. Models tend to favor separate top-level parameters over grouped or composite forms. (2) Descriptions that explain parameters also tend to increase selection, while name effects are weaker and less consistent. (3) Targeted comparisons show that schema effects depend on the specific parameter names and on how the request represents the same information. When only one schema satisfies an explicit request requirement, all five models select the compatible tool despite position preferences. These results show that parameter schemas influence not only how tools are called, but also whether they are selected. For MCP developers and evaluators, schema design and tool ordering can therefore affect which implementation is invoked even when functionality is unchanged.

AI 总结

研究问题：参数模式差异是否影响MCP中LLM的工具选择？ 主要方法：控制元数据因素进行干预实验，使用条件逻辑回归分析位置调整效应。 关键贡献：发现参数模式变化对选择影响最大且最一致，描述解释参数增强选择，名称效应较弱。

Measuring the Inference Energy Efficiency of Open-Weight LLMs across Benchmarks, Reasoning Modes, and Hardware
相关度 ★★★☆☆ (0.38)

作者：Zeyu Yang, Wes Armour

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.14389v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Benchmark scores tell us what an LLM can do, but not how much energy is required to deliver that capability. We directly measure this trade-off for 86 open-weight model checkpoints, evaluated on seven benchmarks and six hardware platforms ranging from a consumer GPU to an eight-GPU server. We find large differences in energy efficiency even among models with similar capability. Most configurations lie away from the score--energy Pareto frontier: they use a median of 2.6--4.8$\times$ more energy than another configuration that scores at least as well, with differences reaching 27$\times$. Reasoning can improve capability, but enabling thinking increases energy by about 3.7$\times$ on average, and the benefit depends strongly on the model family and output budget. Generated-token count is also an imperfect proxy for energy, misordering 18--24\% of model pairs. In contrast, FP8 reduces energy by roughly a quarter with little change in score, while changing hardware largely rescales energy without changing the relative ranking of models. These results show that capability alone gives an incomplete picture of efficiency. We argue that benchmark scores should be reported together with measured energy consumption under a specified serving regime and output budget.

AI 总结

研究问题：测量开放权重LLM在基准测试、推理模式和硬件上的推理能效权衡。 主要方法：对86个模型检查点，在7个基准和6个硬件平台上直接测量能效与能力的关系。 关键贡献：发现能效差异巨大，即使能力相近；推理模式增加能耗约3.7倍；FP8降低能耗约1/4；建议报告能耗与能力。

Echo: Error-Compensated 4-Bit Tensor-Parallel Communication for LLM Training and Inference
相关度 ★★☆☆☆ (0.28)

作者：Xingyu Xie, Xiaoyong Xie, Zhouchen Lin, Kim-Chuan Toh

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.14277v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Tensor parallelism ends every layer with an all-reduce that sits between two dependent matrix multiplications, so its cost cannot be hidden and grows as compute outpaces interconnect bandwidth. Sending 4-bit instead of 16-bit values would cut this cost by nearly 4x, but the all-reduce must add the values it carries, and rounding the sum injects an error at every call. Model quality depends on how these errors accumulate over layers, yet existing methods treat each call on its own. We present Echo, a 4-bit all-reduce for training and inference that controls the accumulated error. Echo moves data in 4 bits and sums in high precision, so each value is quantized at most twice per call for any number of GPUs. The summation reveals each injected error, and Echo feeds it back into later calls, so old errors cancel instead of accumulating. We prove that the accumulated error stays bounded independently of depth. On A100 GPUs, Echo is 2.09x faster than the 16-bit NCCL all-reduce on 1 GB messages. It speeds up the time to first token by up to 1.16x and the training step by up to 1.09x on an idle interconnect, and by up to 1.44x and 1.38x as background traffic grows. It keeps the average accuracy over seven benchmarks within 0.4 points of 16-bit inference on Qwen3-8B. In 10B-token pretraining, its final loss is less than 0.9% above that of 16-bit training. Echo needs no FP8 or FP4 hardware.

AI 总结

研究问题：张量并行中全归约通信成本高，4位量化引入累积误差。 主要方法：Echo，一种4位全归约，高精度求和，反馈旧误差以抵消累积。 关键贡献：证明累积误差有界，加速通信，保持精度。
