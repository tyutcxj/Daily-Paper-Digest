How Many Samples Is RL Worth? Best-of-N versus RL Post-Training for Text-to-Image Models
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.56)

作者：Salil Bhatnagar

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.33232v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Reinforcement learning (RL) on a reward that can be checked automatically has become a common last step in the training of text-to-image models. A simpler way to get more correct images is best-of-N: draw N images from the base model and keep the one that a picker prefers. We ask how many base model samples one RL sample is worth, and call this number the exchange rate. We measure it for Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium and its public Flow-GRPO checkpoint on 398 new prompts in the style of GenEval, the benchmark behind the RL reward, with human labels on every prompt and two automatic judges other than the reward. The answer depends on the prompt. Four fifths of the prompts do not ask for a position (for example, one object left of another). On these, the base model catches up: best-of-16 with an off-the-shelf vision-language model (VLM) as the picker is about as good as one RL image. A person who looks through up to 3 base images finds a correct one as often as one RL image is correct. On the prompts that ask for a position, RL is far ahead: a person finds a correct image among 8 base images for only 52% of the prompts, against 92% for one RL image. Over all prompts, our primary test, which we fixed in advance, finds best-of-16 with the VLM picker 9.3 points behind one RL image when both automatic judges must pass an image. People see a smaller gap, 5.5 and 6.5 points in two checks. FLUX.1-dev shows a similar gap in the same test. RL also solves 20 prompts that the base model misses in 128 tries, and 1,024 tries recover half of them. The automatic judges are wrong in opposite directions: a detector-based judge like the reward, as used in our primary test, reports a larger gap than people see, and VLM judges report little or none. Comparisons of RL with best-of-N should therefore check the selected images by hand.

AI 总结

研究问题：RL后训练与best-of-N采样在文本到图像模型中的效率比较。 主要方法：提出“汇率”概念，通过人类标注和自动评判比较Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium的RL检查点与best-of-N采样。 关键贡献：发现RL在位置提示上显著优于best-of-N，非位置提示上best-of-16可接近RL。

General-Purpose Decision Models for AI Configuration Tuning

作者：Jingzhi Gong, Jie M. Zhang

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.33190v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: AI configuration tuning searches for settings that improve the performance of AI systems under a limited budget. Existing tuners use different sampling and search policies, making the choice of decision strategy central to both tuning quality and cost. We investigate whether general-purpose decision models can guide tuning, using Jev as a deployed representative that returns structured choices and numeric scores across diverse tasks. To assess where it helps, we compare its use as an optimizer, performance predictor, and region guider against conventional methods and a large language model (LLM) on eight AI tuning tasks. Conventional optimizers attain rank 1 in six of eight comparisons. Similarly, established predictors outperform Jev in seven. Through region guidance, however, it finds configurations with lower perplexity than those selected by the native tuner, offering a quality gain when additional decision time and API fees are acceptable. These findings yield actionable recommendations on selecting the decision role, retaining conventional predictors, and balancing tuning quality against cost.

FIG-MAC: A Fine-grained Inspiration Graph Empowered Multi-Agent Collaboration for Automated Scientific Hypothesis Generation

作者：Siyuan Chen, Yuping Gao, Tai Huang, Yuling Zhou, Dongyu Su, Tongguan Wang

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2611.33563v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Automated Scientific Hypothesis Generation (ASHG) leveraging large language models (LLMs) aims to harness their strengths in knowledge integration, logical reasoning, and text generation for efficiently producing innovative, verifiable scientific hypotheses. However, existing approaches face three critical limitations: (i) insufficient fine-grained knowledge representation, as unstructured context impairs LLM comprehension and reasoning; (ii) lack of knowledge evolution traceability, constraining the generation of innovative cross-domain conjectures; (iii) absence of human-inspired and role-specialized multi-agent collaboration, failing to leverage the efficient innovation mechanisms arising from cognitive diversity and division of labor among expert roles to complete the full ASHG-evaluation loop. To address these limitations, we propose the Fine-grained Inspiration Graph-empowered Multi-Agent Collaboration framework (FIG-MAC) including: (i) Fine-grained Inspiration Graph module (FIG), to obtain fine-grained structured knowledge representations, where nodes represent academic semantic units and edges encode heuristic relationships among them; (ii) ontology-guided hybrid reasoning module, which follows the FIG ontology to construct structured reasoning paths and uses vector search to enrich them with semantic evidence, to obtain the traceable scientific conjecture paths; (iii) role-specialized Multi-Agent framework, inspired by real scientific teamwork, enabling a closed-loop process of scientific conjecture generation, screening, and validation. Experimental results demonstrate that FIG-MAC significantly outperforms state-of-the-art methods on source diversity, provenance-adjusted novelty, and raw novelty.

研究问题：现有自动科学假设生成方法存在细粒度知识表示不足、缺乏知识演化可追溯性及多智能体协作缺失。 主要方法：提出FIG-MAC框架，包含细粒度灵感图模块和本体引导混合推理模块。 关键贡献：实现结构化知识表示与角色专业化多智能体协作，提升跨领域假设生成创新性。

Searching Ultra-Long Videos with Decision Models
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.52)

作者：Ce Zhang, Baiqi Li, Seongsu Ha, Gedas Bertasius

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2611.33368v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Understanding ultra-long videos requires retrieving sparse, query-relevant evidence from extensive video histories. Existing similarity-based retrieval methods are efficient, but they encode the question and the video content independently and thus often miss evidence whose relevance requires reasoning over both. Generative large language models (LLMs) can perform such reasoning, but they typically generate text over multiple rounds of search, which makes each query slow and costly. In this work, we introduce DeLoVi (Decision Models for Long Video), a framework that leverages pretrained decision models to retrieve evidence from ultra-long videos. Like an LLM, a decision model reads the question together with the candidate evidence, so it can reason over both. Unlike an LLM, it directly outputs a relevance score without generating text, which makes it fast and inexpensive. We first present DeLoVi-S, a simple method that generates captions for short video clips and uses a decision model to rank those captions by their relevance to a question. A generative LLM then answers the question using the top-ranked captions. Despite its simplicity, DeLoVi-S achieves state-of-the-art performance on three ultra-long video understanding benchmarks, EgoLifeQA, EgoMemReason, and MM-Lifelong, and generalizes effectively to long-video understanding benchmarks, VideoMME-L and LVBench. However, DeLoVi-S scores every caption for every question, so its cost grows linearly with video length. To reduce this cost, we propose an automated policy evolution framework that iteratively refines the memory construction and retrieval policies around a fixed pretrained decision model. The resulting method, DeLoVi-E, maintains comparable performance while reducing input tokens by 34x on average compared with DeLoVi-S. It also runs over 3x faster than the best prior method on EgoLifeQA while achieving higher accuracy.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何高效检索超长视频中与查询相关的稀疏证据。 主要方法：提出DeLoVi框架，用预训练决策模型直接输出候选证据与问题的相关性分数，无需生成文本。 关键贡献：DeLoVi-S方法在三个超长视频理解基准上达到SOTA，兼具推理能力和高效性。

An ablation study isolates the contribution of each component and finds that the largest gains come from combining hard checks with candidate comparison: on tasks where the first sampled answer is often broken or unsafe, this pairing lifts joint correctness-and-safety by up to +0.21 (retrieval-augmented QA) and code-generation safety by up to +0.28 (CWEval), with the size of the gain tracking how weak the first sample is, falling to near zero or slightly negative where it is already strong. The per-task layer that selects and weights the softer checks adds no measurable value over simpler alternatives. This builds a unified scoring framework, together with an ablation that identifies the grounded hard checks, not the soft-factor configuration, as the source of the gain.

arXiv:2611.33237v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Resource-efficient artificial intelligence has become increasingly important for sustainable agriculture, where timely disease forecasting must be achieved under computational and energy constraints. Early estimation of rice blast can enhance food security by optimizing crop management practices while reducing excessive pesticide use. However, existing frameworks often suffer from high computational overhead and limited capability to model the complex spectral-temporal dependencies present in heterogeneous meteorological and remote sensing data. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a lightweight multimodal fusion framework comprising an attentive positional encoding-based Transformer and a convolutional neural network (CNN)-embedded gated recurrent unit (GRU) for efficient spectral-temporal representation learning. The attention-enhanced positional encoding assigns adaptive importance to sequential meteorological tokens, enabling effective modelling of temporal characteristics. In parallel, the lightweight CNN extracts discriminative spectral features, while the GRU captures temporal dynamics from remote sensing imagery acquired over two time-instances. A multimodal fusion layer integrates these heterogeneous representations to forecast rice blast risk 5-10 days before symptom manifestation across diverse agro-climatic zones. Extensive experiments demonstrate that the proposed framework outperforms state-of-the-art methods, achieving a validation accuracy of 96.93% and an F1-score of 95.79%. In addition to its predictive performance, the lightweight multimodal architecture substantially reduces computational complexity, inference latency, and carbon emissions, making it an energy-efficient solution for large-scale disease forecasting. The proposed framework provides a practical foundation for resource-efficient artificial intelligence in sustainable agriculture.
