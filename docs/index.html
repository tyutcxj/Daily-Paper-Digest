OA-RAG: Ontology-based Agentic RAG for Faithful LLM Reasoning
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Hui Yang, Jiaoyan Chen, Yongsheng Gao

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.36335v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) perform well across many real-world tasks but remain prone to hallucination, limiting their reliability in domains such as biomedicine and healthcare, where answers must be grounded in established knowledge. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) mitigates this problem by incorporating external information, but often overlooks logical relationships and relies heavily on the LLM's own reasoning, which can compromise answer faithfulness. We propose OA-RAG, an agent that integrates formal reasoning with LLM reasoning, drawing on both the logical structure and informal annotations of domain ontologies. For each answer, OA-RAG reports the ontology observations used as evidence and a model-assessed grounding level (entailed, supported, related, or unrelated) that characterises the strength of that evidence. Across benchmarks derived from three large-scale ontologies and six real-world medical question-answering tasks, OA-RAG achieves the best overall performance among the evaluated ontology-fine-tuned and popular RAG methods, with particularly strong results on reasoning-intensive tasks. The reported grounding levels also offer a useful indication of answer reliability, with stronger grounding generally associated with higher accuracy. Finally, our results demonstrate the value of ontologies as knowledge sources: OA-RAG is competitive with, and sometimes outperforms, domain-specific RAG systems that draw on sources such as textbooks.

Ontology-guided multi-agent reasoning for explainable differential diagnosis
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.47)

作者：Roxana Ramona Szomiu, George Ursu, Adrian Groza

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.36325v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Effective differential diagnosis depends on well structured and unambiguous patient case descriptions, since incomplete or ambiguous clinical information readily leads to diagnostic errors and inconsistent reasoning. Large Language Models (LLMs) have recently shown promising capabilities in medical question answering and clinical reasoning; however, even when achieving competitive performance on medical benchmarks, they often rely on pattern recognition learned from training data rather than grounded clinical evidence, and can generate plausible yet unsupported diagnostic conclusions. Their reasoning remains largely opaque, making it difficult for clinicians to assess the evidence and rationale behind a given recommendation. To address this gap, we propose an ontology-guided, multiagent differential diagnosis framework that replicates the iterative reasoning process of physicians. The framework grounds diagnostic inference in a structured, expert-curated medical knowledge base, enabling interpretation of symptoms, clinical findings, or disease relationships, while decomposing diagnosis into explicit, auditable reasoning steps. This ontology-based grounding constrains inference to evidence-backed associations, reducing hallucination and improving diagnostic transparency, traceability, and consistency. The system then identifies the most discriminative missing symptoms and generates targeted follow-up questions to reduce uncertainty through a process that mirrors to clinical inquiry. Together, these components improve the reliability, explainability, and diagnostic accuracy of AI-assisted differential diagnosis. The source code is publicly available.

The Trinity:Capability-Driven Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.42)

作者：Weidong Tang, Ruiheng Wang, Jingxuan Zhang, Hexin Wang, Haoyuan Chen, Zhengfeiyang Zhang, Yixing Ma, Zichen Jiang, Ziyu Guo, Haodong Li, Zhengbo Jiao, Zhiqing Cui, Xinxiang Yin, Shuo Liang, Zhenglin Wan, Hongzhan Lin, Wangbo Zhao Kaipeng Zhang, Pengfei Zhou, Odin Zhang, Yang You

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.36077v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multimodal intelligence can be viewed as human-guided co-evolution between worlds and agents through interaction. However, scaling this process faces two bottlenecks: constructing heterogeneous, verifiable environments is costly, and manual Harness iteration cannot keep pace with growing task diversity and complexity. A key insight is that tasks with different modalities and surface forms can share the same underlying capability requirements. We therefore introduce The Trinity, a recursive self-improvement framework coupling world generation with Harness evolution through three roles: Creator, Explorer, and Evolver. Drawing on verified execution trajectories, Evolver shifts the basis of task organization from surface categories to shared requirements, reorganizing the original task tree into a hierarchical capability tree. We introduce Capability-Driven Real2Sim2Real: for heterogeneous tasks at the same node, Creator abstracts their common requirements into simplified, verifiable simulated worlds for capability learning, while evaluation on original tasks measures transfer. Along this capability tree, Explorer progressively evolves reusable capability modules through selective inheritance, forming a Harness tree whose validated updates accumulate across iterations within a fixed topology. The resulting Harnesses specialize in different capabilities, motivating a Capability-Aware Harness Router that uses public task information to select compatible module configurations from the tree. Our evolved Harnesses improve mean performance by 34 points across 28 training benchmarks and 26 points across 18 unseen benchmarks, including two real-world embodied tasks.

研究问题：多模态智能扩展面临环境构建成本高和人工迭代跟不上任务多样性的瓶颈。 主要方法：提出The Trinity框架，通过Creator、Explorer、Evolver角色递归自改进，基于能力驱动将任务树重组为能力树，并实现Real2Sim2Real。 关键贡献：实现能力驱动的递归自改进，降低环境构建成本，提升任务泛化能力。

Skill Dreaming: Evolving Repository Skills through Pull Request Streams
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.42)

作者：Xinyuan Song, Hasibul Haque, Jay Somasundaram, Ryan Turner, Bowen Zhu, Liang Zhao

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.36188v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly rely on reusable skills for complex software engineering tasks, making skill libraries essential for repository development and maintenance. However, repository-specific skills must evolve with changing code structures, implementation conventions, and maintenance requirements. Pull requests (PRs) provide a natural source of such knowledge by recording how repositories evolve through implementation and review. Meanwhile, new PRs must follow repository-specific implementation patterns, modify related components consistently, and ensure that existing tests continue to pass while adding coverage for new functionality. This creates a mutually reinforcing cycle: PRs provide knowledge for updating skills, while these skills improve future PRs, maintain repositories, and support further skill evolution. To enable this co-evolution of PRs, repositories, and skills, we propose Skill Dreaming, a framework that learns from PR streams to guide continuous repository development and maintenance. Given a repository and its PR history, Skill Dreaming builds a repository-specific skill library by extracting executable knowledge from validated PRs and continuously refines the skills as new PR information becomes available. These skills guide coding agents not only in implementing requested features but also in maintaining associated tests, documentation, compatibility, and repository conventions. By integrating PR analysis, skill management, code execution, and continuous integration, Skill Dreaming provides an end-to-end workflow for applying learned skills to repository changes and incorporating subsequent PR outcomes into future skill updates.

MARQ: A Modular, LLM-Agnostic Requirement Quality Assessor Infused with Systems Engineering Knowledge
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.41)

作者：Jannatul Shefa, Alejandro Salado, Paul Wach, Taylan G. Topcu

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.36234v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Requirements engineering (RE) is foundational to systems engineering (SE), as requirement defects can propagate downstream and adversely affect development outcomes. Although large language models (LLMs) are promising for requirement quality assessment, prior work shows that off-the-shelf models miss a large share of expert-identified defects, perform unevenly across quality criteria, and vary across executions. This study investigates whether infusing expert-derived SE knowledge can improve LLM-based requirement quality evaluation beyond off-the-shelf capability. We present MARQ, a modular, LLM-agnostic prototype that supplements LLMs with a criterion-specific knowledge stack operationalizing INCOSE requirement quality criteria and evaluated against off-the-shelf baselines on an expert-labeled NASA requirement set using Claude Opus 5, GPT-5.6 Sol, and the open-weight Gemma 3 12B, with 100 executions per model. MARQ achieves median F1 scores of 0.80 for Claude Opus 5, 0.62 for GPT-5.6 Sol, and 0.53 for Gemma 3 12B, compared with off-the-shelf baselines of 0.55, 0.52, and 0.18, respectively. However, the nature of these gains is model-dependent. Claude Opus 5 improves defect detection and false-alarm control; GPT-5.6 Sol gains detection and better execution-level consistency at the cost of more false alarms; and Gemma 3 12B reaches the performance of off-the-shelf proprietary models, although it remains highly variable. Criterion-level analysis shows gains concentrated in Correctness and Conformance, trade-offs between misses and false alarms within individual criteria, and model-dependent responses across several criteria. These findings position SE knowledge infusion as a promising model-agnostic pathway for strengthening trustworthy LLM-based RE support while underscoring the need for criterion-specific interventions and human-in-the-loop review.

研究问题：如何提升LLM在需求质量评估中的缺陷检测能力。 主要方法：提出MARQ模块化原型，注入INCOSE标准知识栈，评估多个LLM。 关键贡献：MARQ显著提升F1分数，但增益依赖模型特性。

WAKMan: Workload Aware KVCache Manager
相关度 ★★★☆☆ (0.38)

作者：Suhas Kumar Morisetty, Vaibhavi Lokegaonkar, Sachin Mathew Varghese, Wesley Harris

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.36355v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Prefix caching enables a Large Language Model (LLM) servers to reuse the Key-Value (KV) states and accelerate prefill computation stage for requests that share a prefix with earlier prompts, thus reducing latency and serving cost. With limited memory resource, the KV cache eviction policy becomes a decisive factor in determining the overall benefits of prefix caching. Production traffic may have single-turn, multi-turn, agentic, or a mixture of any combination of these request types. Our findings indicate that agentic steps return within seconds and favor recency, while popular prompts and long conversations return after many other requests and favor frequency. Across 20 mixes, no single policy is always best: vLLM's default strategy LRU provides 12% higher cache hits as compared to the frequency-oriented S3FIFO on agentic-heavy mixes but trails it by up to 22% elsewhere, and even a single workload parameter can flip the winner even for any particular mix. We present WAKMan, a workload-aware KV cache manager that picks its eviction policy from live traffic without knowing request types or reading prompts. It improves hit rate over LRU by 5 percentage points on average, with the lowest mean and worst-case optimality gap of all policies.

AI 总结

研究问题：KV缓存管理中不同请求类型导致单一驱逐策略效果不佳，需工作负载感知管理。 主要方法：提出WAKMan，从实时流量自动选择驱逐策略，无需请求类型或提示信息。 关键贡献：相比LRU平均命中率提升5个百分点，最低平均和最差情况最优性差距。

How Stable Are LLM-Generated Proofs Under Program Refactoring? A Stress Test for Verifiable Code Generation
相关度 ★★★☆☆ (0.33)

作者：Aravind Mandiga, Guoming Li

发布日期：Thu,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.36328v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Benchmarks for verifiable code generation usually test a proof against one fixed implementation. We ask whether the same proof remains valid after a program refactoring that preserves behavior. We introduce RefactorProof, which derives 458 Lean-certified variants from the 185 VERINA tasks that compile in our pinned environment while holding the specification and evaluated proof fixed byte-for-byte. VERINA supplies reference proofs for 46 tasks, all from its basic split; the primary model-versus-reference comparisons further match on 21-25 short, non-recursive tasks. Structural refactorings expose substantial brittleness across proof sources: task-normalized structural PSR is 4.3% for the supplied references and 13.3-26.8% for the specialized provers. On the matched tasks, specialized prover proofs nevertheless have higher overall unchanged-proof survival than the supplied reference artifacts: +15.6 points for DeepSeek-Prover-V2-7B, +18.8 for Goedel-Prover-V2-32B, and +20.3 for Goedel-Prover-V2-8B; all three 95% CIs exclude zero. The ordering persists after excluding universally survived T1 variants, under family-balanced aggregation, and under alternative deployable valid-proof selection rules. However, unchanged-proof failure is not the same as high repair cost: predefined one-identifier edits repair 51/54 eligible reference structural failures and 58/85 eligible prover failures. RefactorProof therefore measures unchanged-proof stability under program refactoring, not the cost of maintaining a proof after it breaks.

研究问题：LLM生成的证明在程序重构后是否仍然有效？ 主要方法：引入RefactorProof，从185个VERINA任务衍生458个Lean认证变体，保持规范和证明不变。 关键贡献：发现结构重构导致证明脆弱性，但专业证明器的证明存活率高于参考证明。
