Can Personal Requirements Become Supervision for Planning?
相关度 ★★★★★ (0.50)

作者：Junwei Wu, Runze Yan, Yuzhang Xie, Hanqi Luo, Ziwei Dong, Jiaying Lu, Xiao Hu, Carl Yang

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.40992v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Managing multiple chronic conditions through diet requires satisfying disease- and medication-specific requirements jointly across meals and days while preserving established eating habits. These requirements are public in clinical guidelines and drug labels, yet turning them into a feasible plan for one person remains difficult for language-model agents. In our dietitian-approved weekly planning evaluation built from NHANES, thirteen models, from medically fine-tuned 8B models to GPT-6.1 Sol, pass fewer than 3% of complete plans through direct generation. Explicit rules, higher reasoning effort, or model-directed tools leave GPT-6.1 Sol at no more than 8%. We present Noura, which pairs LLM planning with executable personal specifications instantiated from public clinical guidance and individual health and dietary profiles. During planning, the specification grounds foods, optimizes portions, checks the complete multi-day plan, and guides revision. Our key insight is that a specification that rejects a plan can also determine what to learn from it. Noura turns executed plans and contextually credited revisions into supervision for compact models, recovering targets even from trajectories that never achieve complete success. The same specification supplies supervised targets, preferences, and rewards for post-training. On 4,993 held-out participants, Noura-SFT raises Qwen3-14B's complete-plan pass rate from 24.0% to 72.5% within the same execution loop, cuts rule violations by 97.5% with no observed critical failures, and improves dietary quality. The locally hosted 14B planner incurs fewer rule violations than its GPT-6 Luna teacher. A matched comparison shows that executed targets reduce rule violations by 41% over model-written proposals. Across four Qwen, Phi, and Llama backbones, self-training alone reduces rule violations by 41-61% on a 1,249-person population subset.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何将个人饮食要求转化为可行的多日饮食计划 主要方法：提出Noura框架，将LLM规划与可执行个人规范结合，通过规范指导食物选择、优化份量、检查计划并引导修订 关键贡献：规范不仅能拒绝计划，还能决定从中学习什么，从从未完全成功的轨迹中恢复目标

Automatic Construction of Strong Simple Baselines
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Caleb N. Ellington, Jiaqi Wang, Sohan Addagudi, Elijah Cole

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.LG

摘要

arXiv:2610.40700v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: To rigorously establish the value of a novel deep learning method, it must be compared against appropriate baselines. As deep learning has become increasingly common in the physical and life sciences, there have been numerous cases in which apparent progress has turned out to be an artifact of omitted or missing baselines. In this work, we investigate whether this problem can be mitigated by using large language models to automate the construction of baselines. We assemble 36 supervised classification and regression tasks from peer-reviewed studies in Nature, Science, and Cell-family journals that investigate novel deep learning methods for scientific applications. We then use a language model to construct simple baselines for each task. These automatically constructed baselines are strong, recovering a median of 95.5% (mean 85.7%) of the gap between a trivial predictor and the score of the deep learning model. The median simple baseline matches or exceeds the performance of the deep learning model in 12 studies. We further examine the variation across scientific domains and publication venues and we characterize the effects of test-time compute and access to different levels of study context. As human peer review has struggled to keep up with the rapidly increasing volume of research articles in recent years, our results suggest that automated baseline construction is a practical and scalable check on deep learning contributions in the physical and life sciences.

AI 总结

研究问题：如何自动构建强基线以解决科学应用中深度学习基线缺失问题。 主要方法：收集36个监督学习任务，使用语言模型自动构建简单基线并评估性能。 关键贡献：自动基线恢复中位95.5%性能差距，在12项研究中匹配或超越深度学习模型。

Towards Portable Specification of the Agent Harness
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Nicolas Gertler, Claudio Novelli, Luciano Floridi

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.AI

摘要

arXiv:2610.40917v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models act only through the infrastructure that surrounds them. That infrastructure, the harness, determines what a model can accomplish and how it fails, yet it has no settled definition. To address this gap, we decompose the harness into eight functional components that can be specified and compared across systems, and we propose a portable specification framework that records which model ran in which harness. The record is needed because different harness configurations can yield several-fold differences in reported scores for the same model, and because much of an agent's attack surface lies in its harness. Existing model cards and agent cards provide release and operational information, but do not establish the sequence of effective configurations within a run. Harnesses can change between releases and between runs, and, where agents edit their own tools and configuration, within a run. Enacted AI laws contain documentation duties relevant to covered systems, subject to their respective scopes and application dates, but do not prescribe the common harness record proposed here. Therefore, the harness has to be explicitly described and specified as a versioned part of the system.

AI 总结

研究问题：大型语言模型的“harness”缺乏明确定义，导致得分差异和安全风险。 主要方法：将harness分解为八个功能组件，提出可移植规范框架记录配置。 关键贡献：提出harness的显式描述和版本化规范，弥补现有记录空白。

FieldGuideRAG: Image-Grounded GraphRAG for Fine-Grained Recognition with Vision-Language Models
相关度 ★★★★☆ (0.49)

作者：Jiacheng Ding, Yanliang Qi, Cong Guo, Peihua Mai

发布日期：Mon,

类别：cs.CV

摘要

arXiv:2610.40971v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We present FieldGuideRAG, a training-free GraphRAG method in which a vision-language model, or VLM, recognizes fine-grained species by comparing the query with real photographs stored on the nodes of a knowledge graph. Multimodal GraphRAG systems describe each entity in text, and text conveys little of the visual detail that separates look-alike species. Our graph stores one photograph on every species node and links each species to its relatives, its geographic range and its ecological facts. A frozen VLM verifies the query against the photograph of each candidate node. FieldGuideRAG fuses the verification scores with the range edge and answers questions about the organism from the fact edges of the verified species. On CUB-200, a Wikidata graph and four iNaturalist domains, the node photograph improves verification over the species name on every graph, while text descriptions add little. Verification of the candidate pool exceeds a SigLIP retriever by up to 16 points, the range edge adds 7 to 19 points on iNaturalist, and answering from fact edges is correct on up to 89.5% of 28,820 ecological questions. The verification scores also detect queries whose species is missing from the graph. Graphs, prompts and code will be released.

AI 总结

研究问题：细粒度物种识别中文本描述缺乏视觉细节，难以区分相似物种。 主要方法：提出FieldGuideRAG，在知识图谱节点存储照片，用冻结VLM验证查询与候选照片，融合地理范围边和事实边。 关键贡献：节点照片提升验证效果，验证得分超SigLIP 16点，范围边提升7-19点，生态问题回答准确率89.5%，可检测缺失物种。

arXiv:2610.40970v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Generative audio systems can produce large libraries of personalized bedtime content, but ordinary media objectives (retention, completion, and novelty) are poorly aligned with the goal of supporting a transition toward sleep. Direct evidence for narrated bedtime audio in adults is mixed, and a recent active-controlled trial did not support such audio as a standalone treatment for sleep disturbance. We translate this uncertain evidence into conservative engineering requirements and present a multilingual pipeline for generating and narrating low-arousal sleep stories. The implemented prototype separates deterministic planning from large language model generation, represents content and delivery parameters explicitly, shapes each script through a four-phase attention taper, validates structural and lexical constraints, performs a bounded error-aware retry, and quarantines unresolved outputs. Provider-agnostic text-to-speech synthesis is followed by paragraph-aware joining, two-pass loudness normalization, and a publication gate that re-measures the saved, encoded file against fixed loudness, loudness-range, and true-peak limits. The product data model records stop position, replay, early abandonment, and completion while treating these events as ambiguous behavioral proxies rather than clinical outcomes. We contribute an evidence-to-requirement mapping, a traceable guardrail architecture with explicit acceptance thresholds, technical evaluation criteria, and a prospective protocol for testing sleep-onset effects. No clinical efficacy is claimed; the system is positioned as a relaxation and attentional-support tool whose benefit and possible reliance must be evaluated empirically.

摘要

arXiv:2610.40858v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Training a large language model is compute-bound, and current recipes divide the work between optimizers by hand: orthogonalized momentum for attention and MLP matrices, Adam-style updates for the rest. This division rests on human priors about which layer type deserves which geometry, and it fixes a single global update budget that every parameter block shares. We introduce FARO, a function-space adaptive optimizer that makes the partition of parameters into blocks the unit of decision. FARO gives each block a geometry by its realized counterfactual return, then freezes the routes and reallocates the frozen per-block budget by cross-block equal risk contribution (ERC), the risk-parity principle from portfolio theory. On a GPT-style model trained on FineWeb, the framework gives two results. First, its FARO-Adaptive arm reaches 3.2780 nats at the full schedule, matching NorMuonAndAdam (3.2794), the best hand-designed recipe: it recovers the human prior without hand-setting per-layer geometry. Second, FARO-ERC improves on that arm: it lowers the clean-data loss at every one of 6 seeds, by 0.0136 nats on average, a modest but robust gain. Its sign test, paired test, and confidence interval all agree, and it exceeds the measured run-to-run noise at no added time or memory. It works by trimming the riskiest blocks rather than expanding the safest, and it holds up under a corrupted stream, still lowering the mean loss and winning the seed majority. The source code is available at https://github.com/wxj630/FARO.
